\section{\bt{Evaluation}{实验评估}}
\label{sec:evaluation}
\bi{The evaluation tests the benefits of executable knowledge reuse and shared maintenance. RelBench measures end-to-end reuse across prediction tasks and retraining cutoffs against published agent-memory methods. A tool-call decomposition examines exploration and validation; controlled metric studies examine memory scope, publication checks, and condition sharing. Data-change and concurrency experiments test whether maintenance preserves correct reuse and whether snapshot binding prevents invalid execution. Together, these studies connect the design's representation, validation, and amortization mechanisms to workload-level outcomes.}
{实验检验可执行知识复用与共享维护的收益。RelBench与已发表的智能体记忆方法比较，测量跨预测任务与重训截点的端到端复用。工具调用分解考察探索与验证，受控指标实验考察记忆作用域、发布检查与条件共享。数据变化和并发实验检验维护能否保持正确复用，以及快照绑定能否阻止失效执行。这些研究将表示、验证和分摊机制与整个负载上的结果联系起来。}
