\section{\bt{Discussion and Conclusion}{讨论与结论}}
\label{sec:limitations}
\bi{\system\ turns database exploration into executable metric and feature definitions. Sharing these definitions together with their data conditions, validation evidence, and maintenance reduces repeated exploration while sustaining reuse as data evolve.}
{\system\ 将数据库探索转化为可执行的指标与特征定义。共享这些定义及其数据条件、验证证据与维护工作，能够减少重复探索，并在数据演进过程中维持复用。}

\bi{\emph{Empirical limits.} RelBench uses one three-cutoff sequence per method, so temporal cutoffs do not establish independent-repetition significance. Five of six tasks meet the full-sequence quality margins, and study-adverse retains a \OfAdverseRegression\% MAE regression. The traces combine full-program reuse with optional definition retrieval and contain no explicit feature-composition call; this study does not isolate the contribution of structured definitions. The controlled metric experiments separately test publication and shared maintenance.}
{\emph{实证限制。}RelBench每种方法只有一条三截点序列，时间截点不能作为独立重复来确立统计显著性。六项任务中五项满足全序列质量门槛，study-adverse仍有\OfAdverseRegression\%的MAE回退。轨迹同时包含整份方案复用与按需定义检索，没有显式特征组合调用；本轮没有分离结构化定义的独立贡献。受控指标实验分别检验发布检查与共享维护。}

\bi{\emph{Future work.} We will extend compilation to repeated-role joins and many-to-many bridges and develop incremental feature maintenance. Explicit units, authoritative-source constraints, and checks on filters and value distributions will address the semantic limitations exposed by unit and backup-copy changes.}
{\emph{未来工作。}我们将把编译扩展到同表多角色连接和多对多桥接表，并探索特征的增量维护。通过显式量纲、权威来源约束，以及对过滤条件和取值分布的检查，解决单位与备份副本变化暴露的语义局限。}
