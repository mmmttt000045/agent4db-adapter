\section{\bt{Evaluation}{实验评估}}
\label{sec:evaluation}
\bi{The evaluation separates complete-policy outcomes from controlled mechanism tests. RelBench compares a policy combining task-program reuse and optional feature-definition retrieval with published agent memories. Tool-call and SQL-source traces describe its behavior without isolating the contribution of structured definitions. Controlled metric studies examine memory scope, publication checks, and condition sharing. Data-change and concurrency experiments test maintenance and snapshot validity within their stated boundaries.}
{实验区分完整策略的结果与受控机制检验。RelBench将整份任务方案复用与按需特征定义检索的组合策略，同已发表记忆方法比较。工具调用和SQL来源轨迹描述其行为，但未分离结构化定义的贡献。受控指标实验考察记忆作用域、发布检查与条件共享；数据变化和并发实验在各自明确的范围内检验维护与快照有效性。}
