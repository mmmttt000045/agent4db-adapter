\Description{Four line charts compare full recheck, SQL/version cache, and MAVRA. Columns separate staggered and simultaneous arrivals and share row-wise axis ranges. Sharing factors are 1, 2, 4, and 8 definitions per table pair. SQL-cache and MAVRA validation counts largely coincide; the retained no-overlap SQL-cache outlier reaches 96 queries and about 199 percent of full-recheck database time. Error bars are observed ranges, not confidence intervals.}
