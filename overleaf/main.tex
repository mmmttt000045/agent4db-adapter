\begin{abstract}
\bi{LLM data agents answer business questions by exploring schemas, probing values, and composing SQL. This work is highly redundant: in our traces, fresh agent sessions spend two thirds of their database calls on lookups, and 93\% of those lookups rediscover a fact that an earlier session already obtained, whereas application workloads repeat stable query templates. Sharing the metric definitions that agents learn removes this redundancy and keeps answers consistent, but it turns each definition into long-lived state over a changing database. A definition's SQL is correct only while data properties hold that the SQL does not state: a filtered key identifies one business record, joins do not fan out, and dates join through the intended role. A data-only update can break these properties without changing the schema, so the query still runs and silently returns a wrong aggregate. We present \system, a middleware that makes executable validity conditions the shared maintenance unit for metric definitions. \system\ admits definitions extracted from judged-successful trajectories, keys their conditions by dependency versions, rechecks only conditions whose tables changed, reuses verdicts across definitions and agents, revokes and repairs failed definitions with regression tests, and checks declared revisions at execution. In a controlled benchmark with 19 definitions and eight agents, condition-level maintenance uses \MaintStagMin--\MaintStagMax\% of the database time of definition-level revalidation under staggered arrivals and \MaintSimMin--\MaintSimMax\% under simultaneous arrivals, with equal correctness. Across ten data-change scenarios and three LLMs, maintained sharing raises accuracy from \ScenAccNoShare\% without shared definitions to \ScenAccCond\%, never uses a stale definition under the modeled breaking changes, where schema-only maintenance and unguarded sharing do so in \ScenStaleModeledSchema\ and \ScenStaleModeledNoguard\ tasks, and needs \ScenMaintRatio\% of the maintenance database time of definition-level revalidation.}
{大语言模型数据智能体通过探索表结构、探查取值和编写 SQL 回答业务问题。这一过程高度重复：在我们的轨迹中，全新的智能体会话把三分之二的数据库调用用于查找，其中 93\% 的查找重新获取了此前会话已经得到的事实；应用负载重复的则是稳定的查询模板。共享智能体学到的指标定义可以消除这类重复并保持答案口径一致，但也使每个定义成为变化数据库上的长期状态。定义的 SQL 只在一些 SQL 本身没有写明的数据性质成立时才正确：过滤后的键对应一条业务记录，连接不放大行数，日期按预期角色关联。纯数据更新可以在不改表结构的情况下破坏这些性质，查询照常执行，却静默返回错误的汇总值。本文提出 \system，一个把可执行有效性条件作为共享指标定义维护单位的中间层。\system\ 只准入从判题成功轨迹中提炼的定义，按依赖版本索引定义的条件，只重查依赖表发生变化的条件，跨定义和智能体复用条件结论，对失效定义撤销并做经过回归测试的修复，并在执行端核对声明的定义修订。在 19 个定义、8 个智能体的受控实验中，正确性相同时，条件级维护的数据库时间在错峰到达时为定义级重验证的 \MaintStagMin--\MaintStagMax\%，同时到达时为 \MaintSimMin--\MaintSimMax\%。在 10 种数据变化场景和 3 个大模型上，维护后的共享定义把正确率从不共享时的 \ScenAccNoShare\% 提高到 \ScenAccCond\%，在已建模的破坏性变化下从不使用过期定义，而只看结构的维护和无守护共享分别在 \ScenStaleModeledSchema\ 和 \ScenStaleModeledNoguard\ 道题中使用了过期定义；其维护数据库时间为定义级重验证的 \ScenMaintRatio\%。}
\end{abstract}

\section{\bt{Introduction}{引言}}
\label{sec:introduction}
\bi{LLM-based data agents are an emerging and rapidly growing class of data-system clients~\cite{luo2026dataagents}, expected to become the dominant workload of data systems~\cite{liu2026agents}. To answer a question such as ``What was the store return amount in September 2002?'', an agent lists tables, inspects columns and values, validates candidate joins, and composes SQL over many turns. The hard part is rarely SQL syntax; it is business semantics. Enterprise warehouses name columns in terminology that only domain experts understand~\cite{floratou2024nl2sql}, and an o1-preview agent solves only 21.3\% of real enterprise text-to-SQL workflows~\cite{lei2025spider2}. Business knowledge closes much of the gap: attaching expert-annotated external-knowledge evidence raises GPT-4's execution accuracy on the BIRD test set from 34.9\% to 54.9\%~\cite{li2023bird}, and answering enterprise insurance questions over a knowledge graph that captures business semantics raises GPT-4's accuracy from 16.7\% to 54.2\%~\cite{sequeda2024kg}.}
{基于大语言模型的数据智能体是一类新兴且快速增长的数据系统使用者~\cite{luo2026dataagents}，并被预期成为数据系统的主要负载~\cite{liu2026agents}。为回答“2002 年 9 月门店退货金额是多少”这样的问题，智能体需要多轮列出表、查看列和取值、验证候选连接并编写 SQL。难点很少在 SQL 语法，而在业务语义。企业数仓的列名使用只有领域专家才懂的术语~\cite{floratou2024nl2sql}；在真实企业 Text-to-SQL 工作流上，基于 o1-preview 的智能体只能完成 21.3\%~\cite{lei2025spider2}。业务知识能弥补很大一部分差距：在 BIRD 测试集上附加专家标注的外部知识证据，使 GPT-4 的执行正确率从 34.9\% 提高到 54.9\%~\cite{li2023bird}；在刻画业务语义的知识图谱上回答企业保险问题，使 GPT-4 的正确率从 16.7\% 提高到 54.2\%~\cite{sequeda2024kg}。}

\bi{Metric definitions carry much of this semantics. A definition fixes which fact rows to aggregate, which records count as one business event, and how measurements relate across tables. Data warehouses have long required such definitions to be conformed across uses~\cite{kimball2013toolkit}. Agents need not rely on hand-written definitions: systems derive domain knowledge, including the calculation logic of derived columns, from script histories and lineage~\cite{weng2025datalab}, organize prior execution trajectories as reusable semantic memory~\cite{biswal2026agentsm}, and, more generally, distill reusable workflows and insights from past experience~\cite{wang2025awm,zhao2024expel}. Sharing a learned definition across agents amortizes exploration, and agent work is highly redundant: across 50 independent GPT-4o-mini attempts per BIRD question, the distinct sub-plans of each size are often less than 10--20\% of the total~\cite{liu2026agents}. Our traces show the same redundancy at the database interface: 93\% of the lookups and checks issued by fresh agent sessions re-derive a fact that an earlier session had already obtained, and with only three concurrent agents, 91\% of lookups are duplicated by another agent within 60\,s (Section~\ref{sec:workload-characterization}). Sharing also keeps answers consistent: one agent's verified meaning of ``return amount'' becomes every agent's.}
{指标定义承载了其中大部分语义。一个定义规定聚合哪些事实行、哪些记录算作一次业务事件，以及度量如何跨表关联。数据仓库长期要求这类定义在各处保持一致（conformed）~\cite{kimball2013toolkit}。智能体不必依赖人工编写的定义：已有系统从脚本历史和血缘中推导领域知识（包括派生列的计算逻辑）~\cite{weng2025datalab}，把既往执行轨迹组织成可复用的语义记忆~\cite{biswal2026agentsm}，更一般的智能体记忆工作则从经验中提炼可复用的工作流和洞见~\cite{wang2025awm,zhao2024expel}。在智能体之间共享学到的定义能摊薄探索，而智能体的工作高度重复：GPT-4o-mini 在 BIRD 的每道题上独立尝试 50 次，各种规模的不同子计划往往只占总数的 10--20\% 以下~\cite{liu2026agents}。我们的轨迹在数据库接口上观察到同样的重复：全新智能体会话发出的查找与检查中，93\% 重新获取了此前会话已经得到的事实；即使只有 3 个并发智能体，也有 91\% 的查找在 60 秒内被另一个智能体重复（第~\ref{sec:workload-characterization} 节）。共享还能保持答案一致：一个智能体验证过的“退货金额”含义，成为所有智能体的含义。}

\bi{Sharing, however, turns a learned definition into long-lived state over a changing database. Liu et al.\ propose that data systems keep a persistent agentic memory store for grounding agents, and caution that if the store drifts from the data, ``the stale information may lead a new probe to make a mistake''~\cite{liu2026agents}; a tutorial on data agents lists adapting to changing data among their open problems~\cite{luo2026dataagents}, and a tutorial on agentic memory notes that memory stores accumulate redundant, outdated, or conflicting information~\cite{li2026agenticmemory}. For metric definitions the risk is specific. A definition's SQL is correct only while certain data properties hold: the filtered key identifies one business record, joins do not fan out, and dates join through the intended role. The first two are instances of the summarizability conditions that govern correct aggregation~\cite{lenz1997summarizability,mazon2009summarizability}; the third fixes which date relation assigns a fact to a period. The SQL text does not state them and the schema does not enforce them. Dimensional modeling treats them as design contracts: the declared grain is ``a binding contract'', ``different grains must not be mixed in the same fact table'', and a date dimension can play several roles in one fact table~\cite{kimball2013toolkit}. Yet routine warehouse operations change the data behind these contracts: type-2 dimensions add a row for each changed member, late-arriving facts and reloads append rows, and a status history appended to a transaction table mixes two grains~\cite{kimball2013toolkit}. Like the soft constraints exploited by query optimizers~\cite{godfrey2001soft}, the conditions hold on the current data and can be broken by the next load.}
{然而，共享使学到的定义成为变化数据库上的长期状态。Liu 等提出数据系统应保存持久的智能体记忆库，为智能体提供依据，并提醒若记忆库与数据不一致，“过期信息可能导致新的探查出错”~\cite{liu2026agents}；关于数据智能体的教程把适应不断变化的数据列为开放问题~\cite{luo2026dataagents}，关于智能体记忆的教程也指出记忆库会积累冗余、过时或相互冲突的信息~\cite{li2026agenticmemory}。对指标定义而言，这一风险有具体形态。定义的 SQL 只在某些数据性质成立时才正确：过滤后的键对应一条业务记录，连接不放大行数，日期按预期角色关联。前两项是决定汇总正确性的可汇总性条件的具体实例~\cite{lenz1997summarizability,mazon2009summarizability}，第三项规定由哪种日期关系把事实归入某个期间。SQL 文本不写明它们，表结构也不强制它们。维度建模把它们当作设计契约：声明的粒度是“具有约束力的契约”，“同一事实表中不得混合不同粒度”，一个日期维度可以在同一事实表中扮演多个角色~\cite{kimball2013toolkit}。然而数仓的日常操作会改变这些契约背后的数据：第 2 类缓慢变化维为每个变化的成员追加一行，迟到事实与重新装载追加行，在交易表中追加状态流水则混入了第二种粒度~\cite{kimball2013toolkit}。与查询优化器利用的软约束一样~\cite{godfrey2001soft}，这些条件在当前数据上成立，却可能被下一次装载破坏。}

\bi{Consider a returns table that stores one completed row per return. An ETL change starts to append an application-state row for each new return. No table or column changes, and the shared definition \code{SUM(sr\_return\_amt)} still executes. It now counts many returns twice: in our experiment a single-period query returns 6,588,699.86 instead of 3,294,349.93. Nothing fails loudly. Such failures are familiar from data pipelines: upstream feeds change in unexpected ways and downstream applications break silently~\cite{song2021autovalidate}, and a seemingly innocuous upstream change can produce data that ``looks perfectly fine'' to its consumer~\cite{breck2019validation}. Agents rarely catch such breaks on their own: in our traces, only 16\% of sessions check key uniqueness, and every wrong answer came from SQL that ran without error (Section~\ref{sec:workload-characterization}).}
{例如，退货表对每笔退货保存一条完成记录。一次 ETL 改动开始为每笔新退货追加一条申请状态记录。表和列都没有变化，共享定义 \code{SUM(sr\_return\_amt)} 照常执行，却把许多退货计算了两次：在我们的实验中，一个单期查询返回 6,588,699.86，正确值是 3,294,349.93。整个过程没有任何报错。这类失效在数据管道中并不陌生：上游数据源以意想不到的方式变化，下游应用随之静默出错~\cite{song2021autovalidate}；一次看似无害的上游改动，产出的数据在使用方看来可能“完全正常”~\cite{breck2019validation}。智能体自己很少能发现这类破坏：在我们的轨迹中，只有 16\% 的会话检查键唯一性，而所有错误答案都来自执行成功、没有报错的 SQL（第~\ref{sec:workload-characterization} 节）。}

\bi{Maintaining shared definitions requires detecting data-only failures while retaining valid knowledge after routine writes. Schema-evolution support~\cite{curino2008prism,zhang2025evoschema} reacts to changes of tables and columns, so maintenance keyed on schema changes misses the returns-table break above, and revoking every affected definition forces LLM relearning after benign appends. Definition-level lazy revalidation, which adapts lazy view maintenance~\cite{zhou2007lazy} to definitions, marks affected definitions pending and checks each on first use. Its conditions provide the required checks, but the work is organized by definition. Store revenue, net profit, and return rate all rely on the same sales grain, so checking each definition repeats that condition and may rescan a large fact table.}
{维护共享定义需要检测纯数据失效，同时在正常写入后保留有效知识。模式演化支持~\cite{curino2008prism,zhang2025evoschema}针对表和列的变化，因此按表结构变化维护会漏掉上述退货表破坏，撤销全部受影响定义则迫使系统在正常追加后调用大模型重学。定义级懒重验证把物化视图的懒维护~\cite{zhou2007lazy}用到定义上，把受影响定义标为待验证，并在首次使用时逐个检查。其条件提供所需的验证，但工作按定义组织。门店营业额、净利润和退货率都依赖同一个销售粒度，逐个检查定义会重复执行该条件，并可能反复扫描大事实表。}

\bi{Our key observation is that the validity conditions underlying metric definitions can be maintained independently and reused across definitions. A condition such as ``ticket and item identify one store-sales row'' has an identity independent of the formulas that rely on it and a specific set of table dependencies. Making these conditions first-class, versioned objects lets one check support every definition that requires it. Maintenance then follows the distinct conditions touched by an update, sharing common work across definitions and agents, much as multiple-query optimization shares a common subexpression across queries~\cite{sellis1988mqo}. In our 19-definition workload, 41 condition references reduce to seven distinct conditions.}
{我们的核心观察是：指标定义所依赖的有效性条件可以独立维护，并跨定义复用。像“小票号与商品确定一条门店销售记录”这样的条件，其身份独立于使用它的公式，并具有明确的依赖表集合。把这些条件作为一等、版本化对象，一次检查就能支撑所有需要该条件的定义。维护由此沿着更新触及的不同条件展开，在定义和智能体之间共享公共工作；这与多查询优化在多个查询之间共享公共子表达式同理~\cite{sellis1988mqo}。在本文的 19 个定义负载中，41 个条件引用对应 7 个不同条件。}

\bi{We present \system\ (\textbf{M}etric-\textbf{A}ware \textbf{V}alidation and \textbf{R}euse for \textbf{A}gents), a middleware between data agents and PostgreSQL that gives shared metric definitions an executable validity model and an update-driven maintenance lifecycle. \emph{Admission} extracts structured definitions from judged-successful trajectories with explicit business evidence, then validates their grain and replays their canonical SQL. \emph{Maintenance} selects conditions whose dependencies changed, shares completed verdicts across definitions and agents, applies a supported implication rule, and coalesces concurrent checks. A failed condition triggers revocation and filter-based repair; regression against learning evidence validates the replacement revision. \emph{Execution} checks the revisions declared by a query and rejects revoked or superseded references. Because maintenance operates on structured definitions, it applies equally to definitions that engineers declare in a semantic layer, which at least one commercial metric layer does not validate at runtime~\cite{databricks2026metricviews}; our controlled benchmark admits such definitions directly.}
{本文提出 \system（Metric-Aware Validation and Reuse for Agents，面向智能体的指标感知验证与复用），一个位于数据智能体与 PostgreSQL 之间的中间层，为共享指标定义提供可执行的有效性模型与更新驱动的维护生命周期。\emph{准入}从具备显式业务依据且判题成功的轨迹中提炼结构化定义，再检查其粒度并重放规范 SQL。\emph{维护}选择依赖发生变化的条件，跨定义和智能体共享已完成结论，应用支持的蕴含规则，并合并并发检查。条件失败触发撤销和基于过滤的修复，针对学习证据的回归测试验证替代修订。\emph{执行}核对查询声明的修订，拒绝已撤销或已被替代的引用。由于维护作用于结构化定义，它同样适用于工程师在语义层中声明的定义，而至少有一个商用指标层不在运行时验证这类声明~\cite{databricks2026metricviews}；我们的受控实验就直接准入这类定义。}

\bi{We evaluate \system\ on synthetic retail data with one million store-sales rows. In a controlled benchmark with 19 definitions, eight concurrent scripted agents, and four update events, condition-level maintenance uses \MaintStagMin--\MaintStagMax\% of the database time of definition-level revalidation under staggered arrivals and \MaintSimMin--\MaintSimMax\% under simultaneous arrivals, with zero stale uses and zero false revocations for both. End to end, agents built on three LLMs face ten data-change scenarios, from benign appends to status histories, duplicate loads, and unit changes. Maintained sharing raises accuracy from \ScenAccNoShare\% without shared definitions to \ScenAccCond\%, and it never uses a stale definition under the five modeled breaking changes, whereas schema-only maintenance and unguarded sharing use stale definitions in \ScenStaleModeledSchema\ and \ScenStaleModeledNoguard\ tasks. Condition-level and definition-level maintenance answer equally well for the two stronger models (\ScenAccCondModelA\% vs.\ \ScenAccDefModelA\% and \ScenAccCondModelB\% vs.\ \ScenAccDefModelB\%), and condition-level maintenance needs \ScenMaintRatio\% of the maintenance database time. The scenarios also expose the boundary of the approach: duplicate loads and re-keyed dates are detected and revoked but not repaired, and a unit change that no condition models goes unnoticed.}
{我们在含 100 万行门店销售记录的合成零售数据上评估 \system。在包含 19 个定义、8 个并发脚本智能体和 4 次更新的受控实验中，条件级维护的数据库时间在错峰到达时为定义级重验证的 \MaintStagMin--\MaintStagMax\%，同时到达时为 \MaintSimMin--\MaintSimMax\%，两者均为零过期使用、零误撤销。在端到端实验中，基于 3 个大模型的智能体面对 10 种数据变化场景，从正常追加到状态流水、重复装载和单位变化。维护后的共享定义把正确率从不共享时的 \ScenAccNoShare\% 提高到 \ScenAccCond\%，并在 5 种已建模的破坏性变化下从不使用过期定义；只看结构的维护与无守护共享则分别在 \ScenStaleModeledSchema\ 和 \ScenStaleModeledNoguard\ 道题中使用过期定义。对两个较强的模型，条件级与定义级维护的正确率相当（\ScenAccCondModelA\% 对 \ScenAccDefModelA\%，\ScenAccCondModelB\% 对 \ScenAccDefModelB\%），而条件级维护只需 \ScenMaintRatio\% 的维护数据库时间。这些场景也揭示了方法的边界：重复装载和日期键改写能被检测并撤销，却无法修复；没有任何条件刻画的单位变化则不会被发现。}

\section{\bt{Background and Motivation}{背景与动机}}
\label{sec:background}
\subsection{\bt{Application and agent workloads}{应用与智能体工作负载}}
\label{sec:workload-characterization}
\bi{In a parameterized application, query templates and database knowledge reside in code: applications invoke the same queries with different parameters, so millions of queries reduce to thousands of templates~\cite{ma2018querybot}. A fresh data-agent session must acquire that knowledge through schema lookups, data probes, and checks before it can write the query that answers its question. To quantify the difference, we compare two workloads (Table~\ref{tab:workload-characterization}). The application side is Redset~\cite{vanrenen2024redset}, the query metadata of 200 provisioned and 200 serverless Amazon Redshift clusters over three months: 441 million queries, analyzed as complete per-cluster streams. Its template identifier is a feature fingerprint, which the dataset documents as a proxy for query-likeness that overestimates repetition; the dataset is a biased sample of clusters, not a representative view of the fleet~\cite{redset2024dataset}. The agent side consists of 100 fresh DeepSeek V4.1 Flash sessions, each answering one of the 25 questions of Section~\ref{sec:evaluation} with only table listing, table description, and SQL tools, three sessions at a time on one database and with no shared state. SQL templates are libpg\_query fingerprints, which ignore constants and formatting.\footnote{\url{https://github.com/pganalyze/libpg_query}} A rule-based classifier, checked on a random sample of calls, labels each call as a schema lookup, data probe, validation, abandoned attempt, final answer, or failure. A lookup's \emph{fact} is its purpose together with the tables and columns it reads, so differently written SQL that probes the same columns counts as the same fact.}
{在参数化应用中，查询模板和数据库知识保存在代码里：应用以不同参数反复调用相同的查询，数百万条查询可归并为数千个模板~\cite{ma2018querybot}。全新的数据智能体会话则必须先通过结构查找、数据探查和检查获得这些知识，才能写出回答问题的查询。为量化这一差别，我们对照两类负载（表~\ref{tab:workload-characterization}）。应用侧采用 Redset~\cite{vanrenen2024redset}，即 Amazon Redshift 中 200 个预置集群和 200 个无服务器集群三个月的查询元数据，共 4.41 亿条查询，按每个集群的完整查询流分析。其模板标识是一种特征指纹，数据集文档说明它是查询相似性的近似、会高估重复程度；数据集是有偏的集群样本，不代表整个集群群体~\cite{redset2024dataset}。智能体侧是 100 个全新的 DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 会话，每个会话回答第~\ref{sec:evaluation} 节 25 道题中的一道，只有列出表、描述表和执行 SQL 三种工具；三个会话同时访问同一数据库，会话之间不共享状态。SQL 模板使用忽略常量与格式的 libpg\_query 指纹。基于规则的分类器（在随机抽样的调用上人工核对）把每次调用标为结构查找、数据探查、验证、放弃的尝试、最终答案或失败。查找所获得的\emph{事实}由其目的及读取的表和列确定，因此写法不同、但探查相同列的 SQL 算作同一事实。}

\bi{The two workloads repeat different things. Applications repeat queries: long-lived principals issue the same templates, which prepared statements and result caches serve well (34\% of Redset's read queries are answered from Redshift's result cache); Redshift's own analysis finds that in half of the clusters, 80\% of queries are exact repetitions~\cite{vanrenen2024redset}. Agents repeat knowledge. Their SQL changes from session to session, and correct answers to the same question use different templates, yet nearly every session looks up the same tables and probes the same columns, and concurrent sessions do so at the same moment. Two dimensions differ less than often assumed. SQL failures are rare, but answers are wrong without any failure: 31 of the 32 wrong answers occur when the question names a metric without defining it. Validation appears in only 16\% of sessions, and what agents check is key uniqueness, a validity condition of the kind \system\ maintains, rechecked independently in every session. The comparison locates both the work that shared definitions save, namely rediscovered schema facts and business meaning, and the risk they must manage: a reused fact remains correct only while the data it was derived from does not change in the wrong way.}
{两类负载重复的东西不同。应用重复的是查询：长期存在的用户主体发出相同模板，预编译语句和结果缓存能很好地服务它们（Redset 中 34\% 的读查询由 Redshift 的结果缓存直接回答）；Redshift 自身的分析发现，在一半集群中 80\% 的查询是完全相同的重复~\cite{vanrenen2024redset}。智能体重复的是知识。它们的 SQL 在会话之间不断变化，同一道题的正确答案也使用不同模板；但几乎每个会话都查找相同的表、探查相同的列，并发会话甚至同时这样做。有两个维度的差别比通常设想的小。SQL 很少出错，答案却在没有任何报错的情况下出错：32 个错误答案中有 31 个出现在题目只给出指标名而不给定义时。只有 16\% 的会话做验证，而智能体检查的是键唯一性，正是 \system\ 所维护的那类有效性条件，却在每个会话中各自重查。这一对照既指出了共享定义可以节省的工作，即重新获取的结构事实与业务含义，也指出了它必须管理的风险：被复用的事实只有在其来源数据没有发生错误变化时才保持正确。}

\subsection{\bt{Data agents and metric definitions}{数据智能体与指标定义}}
\bi{A data agent interacts with the database through tools: it lists tables, describes columns, validates joins, and runs SQL, and it submits an answer together with the queries that produced it. A metric definition packages what the agent had to discover. Our running example is store return amount: its definition records the fact table (\code{store\_returns}), the measure (the amount of completed returns), the grain (ticket and item identify one return), the time role (return date), persistent filters, and the convention for empty input. Initially, every return has one completed row, so no status filter is needed. Question parameters such as the month are not part of the definition. A return-rate definition additionally joins sales to returns on the same business key and uses sale date to assign the sale to a period; it can share the returns-grain condition with the return-amount definition.}
{数据智能体通过工具与数据库交互：列出表、查看列、验证连接、执行 SQL，并在提交答案时附上产生答案的查询。指标定义把智能体需要摸索出来的内容打包保存。本文以门店退货金额为贯穿例子：其定义记录事实表（\code{store\_returns}）、度量（已完成退货的金额）、粒度（小票号与商品确定一笔退货）、时间角色（退货日）、持久过滤，以及无输入行时的约定。最初每笔退货只有一条完成记录，因此无需状态过滤。月份等题目参数不属于定义。退货率定义还按同一业务键连接销售与退货，并用销售日把销售归入某个期间；它可以与退货金额定义共享退货粒度条件。}

\bi{Definitions come from two sources. Semantic layers let engineers declare measures, keys, and relationships by hand; the declarations document intent but are not necessarily checked against the data at query time. The documentation of one commercial metric layer, for instance, states that a declared join property ``is not validated at runtime'' and that measures return incorrect results if the join fans out~\cite{databricks2026metricviews}. Increasingly, such knowledge is also derived from script and query histories~\cite{weng2025datalab} or reused from agent trajectories~\cite{biswal2026agentsm}. Learned definitions reach many agents quickly, but their assumptions are recorded, at best, only implicitly in the exploration that produced them.}
{定义有两个来源。语义层让工程师手工声明度量、键和关系；这些声明记录了意图，但查询时不一定在数据上检查。例如，某商用指标层的文档写明，声明的连接性质“不在运行时验证”，一旦连接放大行数，度量就会返回错误结果~\cite{databricks2026metricviews}。越来越多的此类知识则从脚本与查询历史中推导~\cite{weng2025datalab}，或从智能体轨迹中复用~\cite{biswal2026agentsm}。学到的定义能迅速传到许多智能体，但其前提假设至多只隐含在产生它的探索过程中。}

\subsection{\bt{How updates break a correct definition}{更新如何破坏正确的定义}}
\bi{Continue the return-amount example. An ETL update adds an application-state row for each new return while retaining the completed row. The shared SQL, \code{SUM(sr\_return\_amt)}, still runs, but the ticket-and-item key is no longer unique and the aggregate counts some returns twice. Revalidation discovers this broken grain. Adding a completed-status filter is a repair candidate: it restores one row per return while retaining the business keys. The filter must also preserve the stated business meaning and pass regression against learning evidence. The return-rate definition needs the same filtered uniqueness to avoid join fan-out, so one verdict can justify both definitions. This progression---a valid definition, a data-only break, a failed condition, and a checked replacement---is the maintenance lifecycle developed below.}
{继续退货金额的例子。一次 ETL 更新在保留完成记录的同时，为新退货追加申请状态行。共享 SQL \code{SUM(sr\_return\_amt)} 仍能执行，但小票号与商品键不再唯一，聚合把部分退货计算了两次。重验证发现粒度被破坏。添加完成状态过滤是一个修复候选：它保留业务键，并恢复每笔退货一条记录。该过滤还必须保留声明的业务含义，并通过学习证据的回归测试。退货率定义也需要同一个过滤后唯一性条件来避免连接放大，因此一个验证结论可以支撑两个定义。从有效定义、纯数据破坏、条件失败到经过检查的替代修订，这一过程就是下文展开的维护生命周期。}

\bi{An aggregate is summarizable only if the grouped records are disjoint and complete and the measure is compatible with the aggregation~\cite{lenz1997summarizability}; violations can make analysis tools return wrong results~\cite{mazon2009summarizability}. For a learned definition, disjointness becomes two executable requirements, filtered key uniqueness and bounded join multiplicity, and completeness becomes a coverage requirement on business keys. A third requirement, the time role, fixes which date relation assigns a fact to a period. Data-only updates break each of them in practice: status-history rows or duplicate loads violate grain, reused dimension keys make a many-to-one join fan out, and late or re-keyed rows shift a fact to another period. Schema-based invalidation observes none of these changes. When such conditions are checked on data instances rather than guaranteed by the schema, a check holds only for the current data and any change requires a new check~\cite{lenz1997summarizability}; instance-level approaches must run summarizability checks for every update~\cite{mazon2009summarizability}.}
{只有当被分组的记录互不相交且完备、度量与聚合方式相容时，汇总才是可汇总的~\cite{lenz1997summarizability}；违反这些条件可能使分析工具给出错误结果~\cite{mazon2009summarizability}。对学到的定义而言，不相交性对应两项可执行要求，即过滤后键唯一性和有界的连接多重性；完备性对应业务键上的覆盖要求。第三项要求是时间角色，它规定由哪种日期关系把事实归入某个期间。纯数据更新在实践中会分别破坏它们：状态流水行或重复装载破坏粒度，被复用的维度键使多对一连接放大行数，迟到或重新赋键的记录把事实移到另一个期间。按表结构失效观察不到其中任何一种变化。当这些条件在数据实例上检查、而不由表结构保证时，检查结果只对当前数据成立，任何变化都需要重新检查~\cite{lenz1997summarizability}；实例层面的方法必须在每次更新时执行可汇总性检查~\cite{mazon2009summarizability}。}

\bi{Each requirement can be checked with a SQL probe over a few tables: a grouped count that finds a duplicated key under the filter, or a comparison of row counts before and after a join. Data profiling discovers such unique column combinations and dependencies, while noting that they are valid only for the instance at a specific point in time~\cite{abedjan2015profiling}; data-validation systems run such checks declaratively over growing datasets~\cite{schelter2018deequ} and validate every new batch of data against a schema~\cite{breck2019validation}. What is missing for agents is the link between a learned definition and the checks that justify it, so that the checks run when the definition's data changes and their outcome governs whether agents may use the definition.}
{每项要求都能用读取少数几张表的 SQL 探查来检查：用分组计数找出过滤后重复的键，或比较连接前后的行数。数据画像能够发现这类唯一列组合和依赖，同时指出它们只对某一时刻的数据实例成立~\cite{abedjan2015profiling}；数据验证系统能在不断增长的数据集上声明式地执行这类检查~\cite{schelter2018deequ}，并按模式验证每个新数据批次~\cite{breck2019validation}。智能体场景中缺少的是把学到的定义与支撑它的检查联系起来：定义所依赖的数据一变，检查就执行，其结果决定智能体能否继续使用该定义。}

\subsection{\bt{Maintenance alternatives}{维护方式}}
\bi{Table~\ref{tab:ladder} compares maintenance strategies on benign appends and data-only breakage. Schema-only maintenance retains the stale definition, while revoke-on-write uses LLM relearning after every write. Definition-level lazy revalidation, adapted from lazy view maintenance~\cite{zhou2007lazy}, preserves correctness through checks and repair. Condition-level maintenance shares condition verdicts across definitions when the condition identity and dependency versions match. Figure~\ref{fig:sharing} shows why the difference matters. For an update of \code{store\_returns}, definition-level revalidation checks every condition of the five returns definitions and three return-ratio definitions, including unchanged sales conditions, 19 checks in all. Condition-level maintenance checks the three distinct conditions that read the updated table and serves all eight definitions from their verdicts.}

\section{\bt{System Overview}{系统概览}}
\label{sec:overview}
\bi{\system\ sits between agents and PostgreSQL (Figure~\ref{fig:architecture}). It exposes tool-style HTTP endpoints for listing and describing tables, finding and checking join paths, looking up metric definitions (\code{find\_metric}), and running SQL (\code{run\_sql}). Agents submit final answers with the query references and arithmetic that produced them, so the middleware records a calculation chain for every task. An in-memory store keeps versioned definitions, join paths, grain entries, and evidence. Version detection reads table fingerprints, DML statistics, and an ETL batch log. The prototype comprises about 9,000 lines of Rust, including 36 unit and integration tests.}
{\system\ 位于智能体与 PostgreSQL 之间（图~\ref{fig:architecture}）。它通过工具式 HTTP 接口提供列出和描述表、查找与检查连接路径、查找指标定义（\code{find\_metric}）以及执行 SQL（\code{run\_sql}）等功能。智能体提交最终答案时附上产生答案的查询编号和算式，因此中间层为每个任务记录一条计算链。内存知识库保存版本化的定义、连接路径、粒度条目和证据。版本检测读取表指纹、DML 统计和 ETL 批次日志。原型约 9,000 行 Rust 代码，含 36 个单元与集成测试。}

\section{\bt{Metric Definitions and Validity Conditions}{指标定义与有效性条件}}
\label{sec:model}
\subsection{\bt{Definitions and evidence}{定义与证据}}
\bi{A definition revision is $m^r=(B,I,C,E,r)$, where $B$ is its business meaning, $I$ its structured implementation, $C$ its set of executable conditions, $E$ its supporting evidence, and $r$ its revision number. The implementation records the fact table, aggregate expression, grain, time role, join references, persistent filters, and empty-input convention. Canonical SQL is compiled from $I$ for any question parameters, giving agents a common representation of the calculation. Evidence includes the source task, the explicit basis of the business meaning, tool results, an independent task-success judgment, and the table versions at learning time. Admission requires this judgment together with the executable gates in Section~\ref{sec:maintenance}.}
{定义修订记为 $m^r=(B,I,C,E,r)$，其中 $B$ 为业务含义，$I$ 为结构化实现，$C$ 为可执行条件集，$E$ 为支撑证据，$r$ 为修订号。实现记录事实表、聚合表达式、粒度、时间角色、连接引用、持久过滤和空输入约定。对任意题目参数，规范 SQL 都由 $I$ 编译得到，为智能体提供统一的计算表示。证据包括来源任务、业务含义的显式依据、工具结果、独立的任务成功判定以及学习时的表版本。准入同时要求这一判定和第~\ref{sec:maintenance} 节的可执行门槛。}

\subsection{\bt{Validity conditions}{有效性条件}}
\bi{A condition $c$ is a predicate over the tables $\operatorname{dep}(c)$ with a check query $Q_c$ that returns a violation witness or nothing. Its identity $\operatorname{id}(c)$ consists of its type, tables, keys, join direction, filters, and null policy. \system\ derives conditions from the implementation:}
{条件 $c$ 是依赖表集合 $\operatorname{dep}(c)$ 上的谓词，附带一个检查查询 $Q_c$，返回违例证据或空。其身份 $\operatorname{id}(c)$ 由类型、表、键、连接方向、过滤和空值规则组成。\system\ 从定义的实现中推导条件：}
\begin{description}
\item[\bt{Filtered key uniqueness}{过滤后键唯一性}]
\bi{$\operatorname{KeyUnique}(T,K,\varphi)$: no two rows of $\sigma_\varphi(T)$ agree on $K$. It is derived from the grain and the fact table's persistent filters.}
{$\operatorname{KeyUnique}(T,K,\varphi)$：$\sigma_\varphi(T)$ 中没有两行在 $K$ 上取值相同。由粒度和事实表上的持久过滤推出。}
\item[\bt{Join multiplicity}{连接多重性}]
\bi{For a join reference $L\to R$ on keys $K$ with filters, each $L$ row matches at most the permitted number of $R$ rows and row loss stays within the recorded ratio. It is derived from each join reference.}
{对按键 $K$、带过滤的连接引用 $L\to R$，每条 $L$ 记录匹配的 $R$ 记录数不超过允许值，丢行比例不超过记录值。由每个连接引用推出。}
\item[\bt{Time role}{时间角色}]
\bi{The fact's date key joins the date dimension many-to-one through the declared role. It is derived from the time specification.}
{事实表的日期键按声明的角色与日期维度多对一关联。由时间规格推出。}
\end{description}
\bi{Repair also checks business-key coverage against an explicit population and tolerance. This check rejects filters that restore uniqueness by discarding required keys, preserving the population represented by the definition.}
{修复还依据显式总体与容差检查业务键覆盖。该检查排除通过丢弃必需键来恢复唯一性的过滤，保留定义所代表的总体。}

\subsection{\bt{Versions, verdicts, and reuse}{版本、结论与复用}}
\bi{The version $V(T)$ of a table combines its schema fingerprint, DML counters, and ETL batch identifier. A verdict is the outcome of $Q_c$ at the versions it read and is indexed by}
{表的版本 $V(T)$ 由结构指纹、DML 计数和 ETL 批次号组成。结论是 $Q_c$ 在其所读版本上的执行结果，以如下键索引：}
\[
\bigl(\operatorname{id}(c),\,V(\operatorname{dep}(c))\bigr).
\]
\bi{A verdict serves every definition that requires the same condition at the same dependency versions. Reuse is governed by the condition's type, keys, filters, null policy, and versions. \system\ also supports a key implication: with identical filters and null semantics, uniqueness on $K$ implies uniqueness on each superset of $K$. These rules let different formulas share executable evidence while retaining the conditions' precise populations and semantics.}
{一个结论服务于所有在相同依赖版本上需要同一条件的定义。条件的类型、键、过滤、空值规则与版本共同决定复用。\system\ 还支持一条键蕴含规则：过滤与空值语义相同时，键 $K$ 上的唯一性蕴含其每个超集上的唯一性。这些规则使不同公式可以共享可执行证据，同时保持条件所指总体与语义的精确性。}

\subsection{\bt{States and the target execution contract}{状态与目标执行契约}}
\bi{A revision is \emph{valid} when its conditions hold at the current versions of their dependencies. A dependency-version change makes it \emph{pending}, a state derived by version comparison that triggers revalidation. Revalidation either refreshes the evidence or \emph{revokes} the revision when a required condition fails. Repair produces a new \emph{candidate} revision, which becomes valid after passing the gates in Section~\ref{sec:maintenance}. For snapshot-bound execution, the target contract for a query $q$ declaring $m^r$ on snapshot $s$ is}
{当修订的全部条件在其依赖的当前版本上成立时，修订是\emph{有效}的。依赖版本变化使其进入\emph{待验证}状态；这一状态由版本比较得出，并触发重验证。重验证要么刷新证据，要么在必需条件失败时\emph{撤销}修订。修复产生新的\emph{候选}修订，通过第~\ref{sec:maintenance} 节的门槛后变为有效。对于绑定快照的执行，在快照 $s$ 上声明使用 $m^r$ 的查询 $q$ 的目标契约为}
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
\operatorname{Admit}(q,m^r,s)\Longrightarrow {}& \operatorname{Available}(m^r,s)\\
&{}\land\bigwedge_{c\in C(m^r)}c(D_s),
\end{split}
\end{equation}
\bi{where $D_s$ is the database at snapshot $s$. The contract links a declared revision's availability to its executable conditions on that snapshot. Canonical compilation expresses the definition's recorded business formula. The prototype implements pre-execution revision checks (Section~\ref{sec:execution}); the snapshot-bound realization of this contract is the extension described in Section~\ref{sec:limitations}.}
{其中 $D_s$ 为快照 $s$ 上的数据库。该契约把声明修订的可用性与其条件在该快照上的成立联系起来，规范编译表达定义记录的业务公式。原型实现了执行前修订检查（第~\ref{sec:execution} 节）；满足该契约的绑定快照执行是第~\ref{sec:limitations} 节介绍的扩展设计。}

\section{\bt{Admission and Condition Maintenance}{准入与条件维护}}
\label{sec:maintenance}
\subsection{\bt{Admission from successful trajectories}{从成功轨迹准入}}
\bi{Candidates are extracted only from tasks with explicit business evidence and a successful judgment. The extractor sees the question, the SQL and derivation of the calculation chain, rejected queries, and metadata, but no business rows or result values. Deterministic gates then check the candidate: G1 business basis, G2 judged success, G3 static well-formedness (columns exist, the measure uses only permitted columns, joins follow validated paths, and required grain filters are present without question literals), G4 grain on the current snapshot, G5 SQL review, G6 replay of the example SQL, and G7 replay of the canonical SQL compiled from the fields. G7 checks what other agents will actually read, so a candidate that omits a filter or hard-codes a parameter fails it. Learning questions supply admission evidence; held-out questions only measure transfer.}
{只从具备显式业务依据且判题成功的任务中提炼候选。提炼器能看到题面、计算链中的 SQL 与算式、被拦下的查询以及元数据，但看不到业务行和结果值。随后由确定性门槛检查候选：G1 业务依据，G2 判题成功，G3 静态合法性（列存在、度量只用允许的列、连接沿已验证路径、包含必需的粒度过滤且不含题目字面量），G4 当前快照上的粒度，G5 SQL 审查，G6 示例 SQL 重放，G7 由字段编译的规范 SQL 重放。G7 检查的是其他智能体实际会读到的内容，因此遗漏过滤或把参数写死的候选会失败。学习题提供准入证据，留出题只用于测量迁移。}

\subsection{\bt{Selective revalidation and reuse}{选择性重验证与复用}}
\bi{On use, maintenance compares the recorded versions of each condition's dependencies with the current versions. Conditions over unchanged tables keep their evidence. For each remaining condition, \system\ looks up a completed verdict for the same identity at the current versions, or one implied by a passed stronger condition; otherwise it runs $Q_c$, and concurrent requests for the same check share that execution. Join references are checked through the shared join entries, so a revised or vanished join path also makes the definition pending. The definition-level baseline uses the same conditions, repair, regression, and in-flight merging but rechecks the entire condition set of the definition and forces join guards to rerun.}
{使用时，维护过程把每个条件所依赖表的记录版本与当前版本比较。未变化表上的条件保留原有证据。对其余条件，\system\ 查找同一身份在当前版本上的已完成结论，或由已通过的更强条件蕴含的结论；否则执行 $Q_c$，对同一检查的并发请求共享这次执行。连接引用通过共享的连接条目检查，因此连接路径被修订或消失时，定义同样变为待验证。定义级基线使用相同的条件、修复、回归和在途合并，但重查定义的全部条件，并强制重跑连接守卫。}

\subsection{\bt{Revocation and regression-tested repair}{撤销与回归修复}}
\bi{A failed condition revokes the revision and records the reason. Repair searches filters on low-cardinality columns to restore a failed fact-table grain or a join path. Candidates must preserve business keys and pass G3--G5 plus G8, which compares canonical SQL against judged learning queries on current data. In the returns example, an application-state filter restores uniqueness but loses returns that never had an application-state row; the coverage check rejects it. The completed-status filter retains the population and proceeds to regression. Repair searches over the same table and key are shared across definitions. Failed repair leaves the definition unavailable until new evidence is learned.}
{条件失败会撤销修订并记录原因。修复搜索低基数列上的过滤，恢复失效的事实表粒度或连接路径。候选必须保留业务键，并通过 G3--G5 及 G8；G8 在当前数据上把规范 SQL 与经过判题的学习查询比较。退货例子中，申请状态过滤恢复了唯一性，却丢掉从未有过申请状态行的退货，覆盖检查将其拒绝。完成状态过滤保留总体，进入回归测试。同一张表、同一个键上的修复搜索在定义之间共享。修复失败的定义保持不可用，直到学到新的证据。}

\bi{The triggering request can complete repair and receive the replacement revision. Concurrent requests that observe the revoked revision are rejected while repair runs. Section~\ref{sec:availability} measures this publication window and its coordination cost.}
{触发请求可以完成修复并获得替代修订。修复期间看到撤销状态的并发请求会被拒绝。第~\ref{sec:availability} 节测量这一发布窗口及其协调代价。}

\section{\bt{Revision-Aware Execution}{修订感知执行}}
\label{sec:execution}
\bi{Revision-aware execution connects definition lookup to query use. Agents declare the metric keys and revisions used by a query; the middleware checks those references at execution and rejects absent, revoked, inaccessible, or superseded revisions. SQL review checks the supported grain and join requirements. Explicit rechecking at use makes a definition's maintained state actionable, and revocation notices reach agents on their next tool response. Canonical compilation supplies the calculation encoded by the recorded fields.}
{修订感知执行把定义查找与查询使用联系起来。智能体声明查询所用的指标键和修订号，中间层在执行时核对这些引用，拒绝不存在、已撤销、不可访问或已被替代的修订。SQL 审查检查支持的粒度与连接要求。使用时的显式重查使定义的维护状态直接约束执行，撤销通知通过智能体下一次工具响应传达。规范编译提供记录字段所表达的计算。}

\subsection{\bt{Controlled maintenance workload}{受控维护负载}}
\label{sec:controlled}
\bi{To isolate database maintenance, the benchmark admits structured definitions directly through the validation gates. Four families contain six sales definitions, five returns definitions, three return-ratio definitions, and five catalog definitions. Taking up to $k\in\{1,2,4,6\}$ per family yields 4, 8, 15, and 19 definitions; $k=1$ retains sharing between the sales and return-ratio families. After each update, eight scripted agents each use every definition in shuffled order, with staggered or simultaneous arrivals. Five policies, four sharing levels, and two arrival modes yield 40 cells. We run all cells under forward and reversed policy orders, yielding 80 measurements that control for policy order. The four update events are three benign appends (sales, returns, and catalog sales) and a grain-breaking update, v2, which adds 170,816 application-state rows for returns dated from 2001-10-01 under the existing schema; it breaks ticket-and-item uniqueness, and repair adds a completed-status filter.}
{为单独测量数据库维护，基准把结构化定义直接通过验证门槛准入。四个族包含 6 个销售、5 个退货、3 个退货比率及 5 个目录指标。每族最多取 $k\in\{1,2,4,6\}$ 条，对应 4、8、15 和 19 个定义；$k=1$ 保留销售与退货比率族之间的共享。更新后，8 个脚本智能体各按打乱的顺序使用所有定义，采用错峰或同时到达。5 种策略、4 个共享度和 2 种到达方式构成 40 组。全部配置按正反策略顺序运行，共得到 80 次用于控制策略顺序的测量。四次更新包括三次正常追加（门店销售、退货和目录销售）与一次破坏粒度的更新 v2：v2 在现有表结构下为 2001-10-01 起的退货追加 170,816 条申请状态行，破坏小票与商品键上的唯一性，修复添加完成状态过滤。}

\bi{Database time sums actual operations during post-update uses, including maintenance and version reads. Admission precedes this measurement window. Maintenance waiting sums the time spent by uses that perform or join maintenance, including aggregate agent waiting under simultaneous arrivals. A stale use returns a revision as valid whose canonical SQL fails a current-data probe. False revocations and unavailable uses are counted separately.}
{数据库时间累计更新后使用期间的实际操作，包含维护及版本读取；准入发生在该测量窗口之前。维护等待累计执行或合并维护的使用耗时，同时到达时累计各智能体的等待。过期使用指返回为有效的修订，其规范 SQL 未通过当前数据探测。误撤销与不可用使用分别计数。}

\subsection{\bt{Correctness under updates}{更新下的正确性}}
\bi{All three revalidation methods preserve valid definitions and reject stale ones: they record zero stale uses and zero false revocations across the maintenance cells. Schema-only maintenance returns stale definitions after v2, with 16, 32, 56, and 64 uses per pass at the four sharing levels. Revoke-on-write discards 5, 10, 18, and 22 still-valid revisions across the benign events per cell. Condition-level maintenance therefore retains the observed correctness of definition-level revalidation while enabling the work sharing measured next.}
{三种重验证方法都保留有效定义并阻止过期使用，在全部维护配置中记录零过期使用、零误撤销。只看结构的维护在 v2 后返回过期定义，四个共享度每轮分别有 16、32、56、64 次过期使用。写入即撤销在各组正常事件中丢弃 5、10、18、22 个仍有效修订。因此，条件级维护保留了定义级重验证在实验中表现出的正确性，同时支持下文测量的工作共享。}

\subsection{\bt{Concurrent repair availability}{并发修复可用性}}
\label{sec:availability}
\bi{Concurrent repair reveals a publication-coordination bottleneck. At $k=6$, across two passes, condition-level maintenance rejects 43 of 304 post-v2 uses during repair, versus 24 for definition-level maintenance; staggered arrivals have zero unavailable uses. Requests encountering a revoked revision during repair need coordination with replacement publication. A per-revision wait queue that resumes requests after repair is the next implementation step.}
{并发修复揭示了替代修订发布时的协调瓶颈。$k=6$ 时，两轮合计 304 次 v2 后使用中，条件级在修复期间拒绝 43 次，定义级拒绝 24 次；错峰到达为零不可用使用。修复期间遇到撤销修订的请求需要与替代修订的发布协调。下一步实现是按修订等待，并在修复完成后恢复请求。}

\subsection{\bt{Data-change scenarios with LLM agents}{大模型智能体的数据变化场景}}
\label{sec:scenarios}
\bi{\emph{Protocol.} Five metrics, store revenue, store return amount, store return rate, catalog revenue, and electronics store revenue, each have two learning questions and three scored questions: a new period, a period difference, and a monthly ranking. Agent A answers the learning questions with explicit business definitions; the extractor turns judged-successful trajectories into candidates, which pass admission. Agent B, which has never seen the metrics, answers the scored questions given only metric names, first on unchanged data and then once per data-change scenario. Each scenario starts from the same initial snapshot and shared store, applies one update, re-asks the questions whose metric reads an updated table, and is rolled back. The reference answers reflect the update's business effect, computed before representation changes such as duplicate rows, status rows, or old versions are applied; an agent must therefore filter those rows out to answer correctly. Table~\ref{tab:scenarios} groups the ten scenarios by the condition they target; the unit change is a control that no condition models.}
{\emph{实验流程。}5 个指标为门店营业额、门店退货金额、门店退货率、目录渠道营业额和电子品类门店营业额，各有 2 道学习题和 3 道计分题（新期间、跨期差值和按月排名）。智能体 A 在给出显式业务定义的学习题上作答，提炼器把判题成功的轨迹转成候选，经准入后发布。从未接触过这些指标的智能体 B 只拿到指标名，先在未变化的数据上回答计分题，再在每个数据变化场景下各答一次。每个场景都从相同的初始快照和共享知识库出发，施加一次更新，只重问读到被更新表的指标，结束后回滚。标准答案反映更新的业务效果，在施加重复行、状态行或旧版本等表示变化之前计算，因此智能体必须过滤掉这些行才能答对。表~\ref{tab:scenarios} 按目标条件对 10 个场景分组，其中单位变化是任何条件都没有刻画的对照。}

\bi{Agent and extractor use the same model: DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, GLM-5.3, or GLM-5.3 Flash, served through the Cline gateway with high reasoning effort, a 20-turn limit, and a 60\,s SQL timeout. Each model and method is planned for three repetitions, but the provider's usage cap interrupted part of the runs. We analyze \ScenCells\ cells. Tasks that failed with provider errors (\ScenQuotaTasks) are excluded, leaving \ScenValidTasks\ scored tasks; \ScenCellsDropped\ cells whose learning phase was interrupted are dropped because they never acquired the definitions. Accuracies are computed per model and then averaged, and summary rows weight each setting equally.}

\section{\bt{Related Work}{相关工作}}
\label{sec:related}
\paragraph{\bt{Data agents and business semantics}{数据智能体与业务语义}}
\bi{Agent-first data systems identify redundant agent requests and propose a persistent agentic memory that must track changes to data and metadata~\cite{liu2026agents}; tutorials survey data agents, including their difficulty with changing data~\cite{luo2026dataagents}, and the management of agentic memory~\cite{li2026agenticmemory}. Text-to-SQL benchmarks and practitioner studies show that realistic enterprise schemas, their terminology, and their documentation remain hard for current systems~\cite{lei2025spider2,floratou2024nl2sql}, and that external knowledge and explicit business semantics raise accuracy~\cite{li2023bird,huo2026birdinteract,sequeda2024kg}. Business-intelligence platforms maintain term definitions~\cite{jiang2025siriusbi} or generate calculation logic from script history and lineage~\cite{weng2025datalab}; AgentSM organizes trajectories as structured semantic memory~\cite{biswal2026agentsm}. \system\ adds a maintenance lifecycle to shared learned definitions: executable conditions connect their recorded meaning and implementation to current database state, and updates trigger revalidation, revocation, and repair. Schema and data-model robustness studies~\cite{zhang2025evoschema,furst2025datamodel} address complementary schema and data-model variation; our evaluation isolates data-only breakage under a fixed schema.}
{面向智能体的数据系统指出智能体请求高度重复，并提出必须跟踪数据与元数据变化的持久智能体记忆~\cite{liu2026agents}；教程综述了数据智能体（包括其难以适应变化数据的问题）~\cite{luo2026dataagents}和智能体记忆的管理~\cite{li2026agenticmemory}。Text-to-SQL 基准与实践研究表明，真实企业表结构及其术语和文档对现有系统仍然困难~\cite{lei2025spider2,floratou2024nl2sql}，外部知识与显式业务语义能够提高正确率~\cite{li2023bird,huo2026birdinteract,sequeda2024kg}。商业智能平台维护术语定义~\cite{jiang2025siriusbi}，或从脚本历史和血缘中生成计算逻辑~\cite{weng2025datalab}；AgentSM 把轨迹组织为结构化语义记忆~\cite{biswal2026agentsm}。\system\ 为共享的学习定义增加维护生命周期：可执行条件把定义记录的含义与实现联系到当前数据库状态，更新触发重验证、撤销和修复。表结构与数据模型鲁棒性研究~\cite{zhang2025evoschema,furst2025datamodel}处理互补的表结构与数据模型差异；本文评估单独考察固定表结构下的纯数据破坏。}

\paragraph{\bt{Agent memory and experience reuse}{智能体记忆与经验复用}}
\bi{Agent memory systems induce workflows from successful trajectories~\cite{wang2025awm}, distill insights or strategies from experience~\cite{zhao2024expel,ouyang2026reasoningbank}, evolve context playbooks~\cite{zhang2026ace}, link and update notes~\cite{xu2025amem}, manage long-term facts~\cite{chhikara2025mem0,packer2023memgpt}, cache plan templates~\cite{zhang2025apc}, and store and reuse past solutions~\cite{guo2024dsagent}. These representations organize knowledge across tasks and conversations. \system\ represents shared metric knowledge together with typed database conditions and versioned evidence. Condition outcomes govern admission and continued use, while a failed condition leads to a new regression-tested revision. This connects experience reuse to the evolving external state on which that experience depends.}
{智能体记忆系统从成功轨迹中归纳工作流~\cite{wang2025awm}，从经验中提炼洞见或策略~\cite{zhao2024expel,ouyang2026reasoningbank}，演化上下文手册~\cite{zhang2026ace}，链接并更新笔记~\cite{xu2025amem}，管理长期事实~\cite{chhikara2025mem0,packer2023memgpt}，缓存计划模板~\cite{zhang2025apc}，并保存和复用既往方案~\cite{guo2024dsagent}。这些表示在任务与对话之间组织知识。\system\ 把共享指标知识与类型化数据库条件、版本化证据一起表示。条件结论决定准入与持续使用，条件失败则产生经过回归测试的新修订，由此把经验复用联系到经验所依赖、不断变化的外部状态。}

\paragraph{\bt{Metric layers and summarizability}{指标层与可汇总性}}
\bi{Dimensional modeling conforms dimensions and facts across business processes~\cite{kimball2013toolkit}, and measures in SQL attach aggregation semantics to tables so that joins do not double count~\cite{hyde2024measures}. Summarizability research identifies disjointness, completeness, and type compatibility as necessary conditions for correct aggregation~\cite{lenz1997summarizability}, surveys how complex multidimensional structures, such as non-strict hierarchies and many-to-many fact--dimension relationships, violate them~\cite{mazon2009summarizability}, and proposes summary constraints analogous to integrity constraints~\cite{horner2004additivity}. Commercial metric layers declare keys and join properties, and at least one documents that a declared join property is not validated at runtime~\cite{databricks2026metricviews}. \system\ turns these conditions into executable checks attached to learned definitions and maintains them as the data changes.}
{维度建模要求在各业务过程间使用一致的维度和事实~\cite{kimball2013toolkit}；SQL 中的度量把聚合语义附着在表上，使连接不再重复计数~\cite{hyde2024measures}。可汇总性研究指出，不相交、完备和类型相容是正确汇总的必要条件~\cite{lenz1997summarizability}，综述了非严格层次、事实与维度多对多等复杂多维结构违反这些条件的方式~\cite{mazon2009summarizability}，并提出类似完整性约束的汇总约束~\cite{horner2004additivity}。商用指标层声明键与连接性质，至少有一家在文档中写明声明的连接性质不在运行时验证~\cite{databricks2026metricviews}。\system\ 把这些条件变成附着在学到的定义上的可执行检查，并在数据变化时维护它们。}

\paragraph{\bt{Constraints, profiling, and data validation}{约束、数据画像与数据验证}}
\bi{Integrity checking specializes verification to updates that can violate a constraint~\cite{nicolas1982integrity,ceri1990constraint}, and derived-relation maintenance can first identify irrelevant updates~\cite{blakeley1989irrelevant}. Soft constraints hold on current data without being enforced and support optimization while valid~\cite{godfrey2001soft}; CORDS discovers approximate dependencies from data samples for selectivity estimation~\cite{ilyas2004cords}. Profiling discovers and maintains unique column combinations and dependencies~\cite{abedjan2015profiling,abedjan2014swan,schirmer2019dynfd}. Data-validation systems check declarative constraints on growing data, share scans~\cite{schelter2018deequ}, and infer constraints~\cite{breck2019validation,song2021autovalidate} or learn acceptable batch profiles~\cite{redyuk2021dynamic} from past data. \system\ couples executable checking to the validity of shared agent knowledge. Conditions are derived from structured learned definitions; versioned verdicts serve every definition that requires them and determine whether an agent may use a revision. Regression-tested repair then restores the definition's implementation when an update breaks a required condition.}
{完整性检查按可能违反约束的更新执行验证~\cite{nicolas1982integrity,ceri1990constraint}，派生关系维护可以先识别无关更新~\cite{blakeley1989irrelevant}。软约束在当前数据上成立但不被强制，并在有效时支持优化~\cite{godfrey2001soft}；CORDS 从数据样本中发现近似依赖，用于选择率估计~\cite{ilyas2004cords}。数据画像发现并维护唯一列组合与依赖~\cite{abedjan2015profiling,abedjan2014swan,schirmer2019dynfd}；数据验证系统对增长的数据检查声明式约束、共享扫描~\cite{schelter2018deequ}，并从历史数据中推断约束~\cite{breck2019validation,song2021autovalidate}或学习可接受的批次画像~\cite{redyuk2021dynamic}。\system\ 把可执行检查与共享智能体知识的有效性结合起来。条件由结构化的学习定义推出，版本化结论服务于所有需要该条件的定义，并决定智能体能否使用某个修订。当更新破坏必需条件时，经过回归测试的修复恢复定义的实现。}

\paragraph{\bt{View maintenance, shared work, and caching}{视图维护、共享计算与缓存}}
\bi{Materialized-view maintenance~\cite{gupta1995maintenance,blakeley1986efficiently} can defer work off the update path~\cite{colby1996deferred} or until a view is referenced~\cite{zhou2007lazy}; our definition-level baseline applies this lazy strategy. Multiple-query optimization and shared execution evaluate common work once~\cite{sellis1988mqo,harizopoulos2005qpipe,giannikis2012shareddb}, while recycling reuses intermediate results and invalidates those affected by updates~\cite{ivanova2010recycling}. Cache designs use semantic descriptions~\cite{dar1996semantic}, update-driven invalidation~\cite{garrod2008ferdinand}, validity intervals~\cite{ports2010txcache}, input-dependent keys~\cite{gunda2010nectar}, and leases that merge concurrent misses~\cite{nishtala2013memcache}. Build systems track dependency hashes~\cite{mokhov2018build}, demand-driven incremental computation recomputes requested dependencies~\cite{hammer2014adapton}, and LLM caches combine reuse with selective recomputation~\cite{agarwal2025cachecraft}. In \system, the maintained object is a condition verdict supporting the recorded implementation of a learned metric definition. Its identity and dependency versions define the reuse boundary across formulas and agents; a failing verdict drives revocation and repair for affected definitions.}
{物化视图维护~\cite{gupta1995maintenance,blakeley1986efficiently}可以把工作移出更新路径~\cite{colby1996deferred}，或推迟到视图被引用时~\cite{zhou2007lazy}；本文定义级基线采用这一懒维护策略。多查询优化与共享执行对公共工作只计算一次~\cite{sellis1988mqo,harizopoulos2005qpipe,giannikis2012shareddb}，中间结果回收复用中间结果，并使受更新影响的结果失效~\cite{ivanova2010recycling}。缓存设计使用语义描述~\cite{dar1996semantic}、更新驱动失效~\cite{garrod2008ferdinand}、有效区间~\cite{ports2010txcache}、依赖输入的键~\cite{gunda2010nectar}及合并并发未命中的租约~\cite{nishtala2013memcache}。构建系统跟踪依赖哈希~\cite{mokhov2018build}，按需增量计算重算被请求的依赖~\cite{hammer2014adapton}，大模型缓存结合复用与选择性重算~\cite{agarwal2025cachecraft}。在 \system\ 中，维护对象是支撑学习指标定义所记录实现的条件结论。条件身份与依赖版本界定跨公式、跨智能体的复用边界，失败结论驱动受影响定义的撤销与修复。}

\section{\bt{Discussion and Limitations}{讨论与局限}}
\label{sec:limitations}
\paragraph{\bt{Evidence and guarantee scope}{证据与保证范围}}
\bi{Business evidence supplies metric meaning, and executable conditions encode the data properties required by its implementation. Repair combines grain and coverage checks with regression against judged learning queries. The reference queries and regression evidence share the synthetic workload generator, so independent business-grounded evaluation remains an important extension. The current prototype checks declared metric references and uses coverage during repair; undeclared use and independently maintained coverage lie beyond this implementation.}
{业务证据提供指标含义，可执行条件编码实现所需的数据性质。修复把粒度、覆盖检查与经过判题的学习查询回归结合起来。参考查询与回归证据来自同一合成负载生成器，独立的业务依据评估是后续扩展的重要方向。当前原型检查显式声明的指标引用，并在修复中使用覆盖检查；未声明使用与独立维护的覆盖条件超出当前实现范围。}
\paragraph{\bt{Proposed snapshot-bound execution}{绑定快照的执行设计}}
\bi{Snapshot-bound execution is a proposed extension for concurrent writers. The current prototype checks before execution, with validation and business SQL on separate snapshots; the evaluation uses controlled update phases. To realize the target contract in Section~\ref{sec:model}, the extension acquires a query snapshot, resolves declared revisions and compatible evidence, validates remaining conditions on that snapshot, and executes on it. Replacements require renewed resolution and declaration. A stable transaction or exported snapshot can support this protocol, and cross-snapshot reuse requires evidence of unchanged dependency data. TxCache provides precedent for consistent cached reuse~\cite{ports2010txcache}.}
{绑定快照执行是面向并发写入的扩展设计。当前原型在执行前检查，验证与业务 SQL 使用不同快照；评估采用受控更新阶段。为实现第~\ref{sec:model} 节的目标契约，该扩展获取查询快照，解析声明修订及兼容证据，在该快照上验证剩余条件并执行。替代修订需要重新解析和声明。稳定事务或导出快照可以支持这一协议，跨快照复用需要依赖数据未变化的证据。TxCache 提供了一致性缓存复用的技术先例~\cite{ports2010txcache}。}
\paragraph{\bt{Generalization and attribution}{泛化与归因}}
\bi{The controlled sweeps isolate maintenance across sharing levels and arrival patterns with eight scripted agents and two policy orders. The end-to-end scenarios cover three LLMs, six methods, and ten updates, but a single synthetic schema, and provider usage caps left the planned three repetitions incomplete, so the comparison for the weakest model remains uncertain. The reference queries and the regression evidence share the synthetic workload generator. The workload study uses one model on this schema; its contrast with Redset is large, but the absolute rates depend on the tools and schema. With WAL and fsync disabled, the reported timings characterize this configuration; durable deployments require separate measurement. A second schema with real update histories and a matched trajectory-retrieval baseline would further isolate the contribution of structured definitions and update-driven maintenance.}
{受控扫描使用 8 个脚本智能体与两种策略顺序，单独测量共享程度和到达方式对维护的影响。端到端场景覆盖 3 个大模型、6 种方法和 10 种更新，但只有一个合成表结构；服务商用量上限使计划的 3 次重复尚未完成，因此最弱模型上的比较仍不确定。参考查询与回归证据来自同一合成负载生成器。负载刻画在该表结构上只用了一个模型，它与 Redset 的差别很大，但绝对比例取决于工具与表结构。关闭 WAL 与 fsync 的耗时刻画当前实验配置，持久化部署需要单独测量。具有真实更新历史的第二个表结构，以及匹配的轨迹检索基线，可以进一步分离结构化定义与更新驱动维护的贡献。}
\paragraph{\bt{Operating range}{适用范围}}
\bi{Two factors determine reuse gains: overlap in definitions' conditions and temporal separation of requests. Shared conditions multiply the uses served by one verdict; staggered requests extend that reuse beyond an in-flight check. Simultaneous arrivals also help the baseline merge work, while updates touching all dependencies reduce scope savings. The measured repair window identifies publication coordination as a separate design dimension. Zero-sharing workloads can quantify fixed overhead. The scenarios mark the model's coverage: duplicate loads and re-keyed dates are detected but cannot be repaired by filters, and value-level changes such as units are not modeled. Repair operators that deduplicate rows or remap keys, and conditions on value distributions, are natural extensions.}
{两个因素决定复用收益：定义所需条件的重叠程度，以及请求在时间上的分散程度。共享条件扩大一个结论服务的使用范围，错峰请求把复用延伸到在途检查结束之后。同时到达也使基线能够合并工作，触及全部依赖的更新则减少检查范围带来的节省。实测修复窗口把发布协调确立为独立的设计维度。零共享负载可以量化固定开销。场景实验标出了模型的覆盖范围：重复装载与日期键改写能被检测，却无法用过滤修复；单位等取值层面的变化没有被刻画。能够去重或重映射键的修复算子，以及关于取值分布的条件，是自然的扩展。}

\section{\bt{Conclusion}{结论}}
\bi{Data agents repeat knowledge rather than queries: fresh sessions rediscover the same database facts that an application keeps in code. \system\ turns that knowledge into shared metric definitions and keeps them correct as the database changes. It represents the data assumptions behind learned formulas as executable conditions, maintains their verdicts by dependency version, and shares that evidence across definitions and agents. Admission, revalidation, revocation, regression-tested repair, and declared revision checks support definitions from initial learning through reuse under updates. In the controlled benchmark, condition-level maintenance uses \MaintStagMin--\MaintStagMax\% of the database time of definition-level revalidation under staggered arrivals and \MaintSimMin--\MaintSimMax\% under simultaneous arrivals, with zero stale uses and zero false revocations. Across ten data changes and three LLMs, maintained sharing raises accuracy from \ScenAccNoShare\% to \ScenAccCond\% and never uses a stale definition under the modeled breaking changes. Changes that no filter can repair, and changes to the meaning of values, mark the next steps.}
{数据智能体重复的是知识而非查询：全新会话反复获取应用早已写在代码里的数据库事实。\system\ 把这些知识变成共享的指标定义，并在数据库变化时保持其正确。它把学习公式背后的数据假设表示为可执行条件，按依赖版本维护条件结论，并跨定义、跨智能体共享这些证据。准入、重验证、撤销、回归修复及声明修订核对，支撑定义从首次学习到更新后的复用。受控实验中，条件级维护在错峰到达时使用定义级重验证 \MaintStagMin--\MaintStagMax\% 的数据库时间，同时到达时为 \MaintSimMin--\MaintSimMax\%，且为零过期使用、零误撤销。在 10 种数据变化和 3 个大模型上，维护后的共享定义把正确率从 \ScenAccNoShare\% 提高到 \ScenAccCond\%，并在已建模的破坏性变化下从不使用过期定义。无法用过滤修复的变化，以及取值含义的变化，是下一步的方向。}
