\section{\bt{Metric Definitions and Validity Conditions}{指标定义与有效性条件}}
\label{sec:model}
\subsection{\bt{Definitions and evidence}{定义与证据}}
\bi{A definition revision is $m^r=(B,I,C,E,r)$, where $B$ is its business meaning, $I$ its structured implementation, $C$ its set of executable conditions, $E$ its supporting evidence, and $r$ its revision number. The implementation records the fact table, aggregate expression, grain, date role, joins, definition filters, and empty-set result. Canonical SQL is compiled from $I$ for any question parameters, giving agents a common representation of the calculation. Evidence includes the source task, the explicit basis of the business meaning, tool results, an independent judgment that the answer is correct, the agent's learning SQL that produced it, and the learning time with the table versions it read. Admission requires this judgment together with the executable checks in Section~\ref{sec:maintenance}.}
{定义修订记为 $m^r=(B,I,C,E,r)$，其中 $B$ 为业务含义，$I$ 为结构化实现，$C$ 为可执行条件集，$E$ 为支撑证据，$r$ 为修订号。实现记录事实表、聚合表达式、粒度、日期角色、连接、定义过滤条件和空集返回值。对任意题目参数，规范 SQL 都由 $I$ 编译得到，为智能体提供统一的计算表示。证据包括来源任务、业务含义的显式依据、工具结果、对答案是否正确的独立判定、产生该答案的智能体学习 SQL，以及学习时刻及其读到的表版本。准入同时要求这一判定和第~\ref{sec:maintenance} 节的可执行检查。}

\subsection{\bt{Validity conditions}{有效性条件}}
\bi{A condition $c$ is a predicate over the tables $\operatorname{dep}(c)$ with a check query $Q_c$ that returns violating rows or nothing. Its canonical form $\operatorname{id}(c)$ consists of its type, tables, key set, join direction, normalized filters, and null policy. \system\ derives four kinds of conditions from the implementation:}
{条件 $c$ 是依赖表集合 $\operatorname{dep}(c)$ 上的谓词，附带一个检查查询 $Q_c$，返回违例行或空。其规范形式 $\operatorname{id}(c)$ 由类型、表、键集合、连接方向、规范化的过滤和空值规则组成。\system\ 从定义的实现中推导四类条件：}
\begin{description}
\item[\bt{Filtered key uniqueness}{过滤后键唯一性}]
\bi{$\operatorname{KeyUnique}(T,K,\varphi)$: no two rows of $\sigma_\varphi(T)$ agree on $K$. It is derived from the grain and the fact table's definition filters.}
{$\operatorname{KeyUnique}(T,K,\varphi)$：$\sigma_\varphi(T)$ 中没有两行在 $K$ 上取值相同。由粒度和事实表上的定义过滤条件推出。}
\item[\bt{Join cardinality}{连接基数}]
\bi{For a join $L\to R$ on keys $K$ with filters, each $L$ row matches at most the permitted number of $R$ rows; it is checked through uniqueness of $K$ on the filtered one side.}
{对按键 $K$、带过滤的连接 $L\to R$，每条 $L$ 记录匹配的 $R$ 记录数不超过允许值；通过过滤后一侧在 $K$ 上的唯一性检查。}
\item[\bt{Date role}{日期角色}]
\bi{The fact's date key joins the date dimension many-to-one through the declared role, as in a role-playing date dimension~\cite{kimball2013toolkit}.}
{事实表的日期键按声明的角色与日期维度多对一关联，即角色扮演维度~\cite{kimball2013toolkit}。}
\item[\bt{Join completeness}{连接完整性}]
\bi{For the date join and every inner join $L\to R$, the fraction of $L$ rows that match no $R$ row stays within the fraction recorded at admission, so that facts are not silently dropped; for the date join we call it date-key completeness.}
{对日期连接和每个内连接 $L\to R$，匹配不到 $R$ 中任何行的 $L$ 记录比例不超过准入时记录的比例，使事实不会被静默排除；对日期连接，我们称之为日期键完整性。}
\end{description}

\subsection{\bt{What the conditions guarantee}{条件保证了什么}}
\label{sec:sufficiency}
\bi{For a period $p$, the canonical SQL of $I$ evaluates $\mathrm{AGG}(e)$ over the rows of $\sigma_\varphi(F)\bowtie R_1\bowtie\cdots\bowtie R_k$ whose role date, obtained by joining the date dimension through $\rho$, falls in $p$. The business meaning $B$ contributes two premises that no query can check: (B1) the business events of the metric correspond one-to-one to the values of $K$ in $\sigma_\varphi(F)$, where $\varphi$ also includes the definition filters on joined dimensions, evaluated on the dimension row that a fact row joins, and (B2) $e$ evaluates on an event's row to that event's measurement. The conditions turn these premises into a guarantee about the computed value.}
{对期间 $p$，$I$ 的规范 SQL 在 $\sigma_\varphi(F)\bowtie R_1\bowtie\cdots\bowtie R_k$ 中、按角色 $\rho$ 关联日期维度所得角色日期落在 $p$ 内的行上计算 $\mathrm{AGG}(e)$。业务含义 $B$ 提供两条任何查询都无法检查的前提：（B1）该指标的业务事件与 $\sigma_\varphi(F)$ 中 $K$ 的取值一一对应，其中 $\varphi$ 也包括被连接维度上的定义过滤，按事实行所连接的维度行求值；（B2）在事件对应的行上，$e$ 求值得到该事件的度量值。条件把这两条前提转化为对计算结果的保证。}
\begin{proposition}[\bt{Sufficiency}{充分性}]
\label{prop:sufficiency}
\bi{If B1 and B2 hold on $D_s$ and every condition in $C(m^r)$ holds on $D_s$, then for every period $p$ the canonical SQL of $m^r$ returns $\mathrm{AGG}$ over the measurements of the events whose role date lies in $p$, each event contributing exactly once; the events it omits are those whose date key or inner-join key matches no row of the joined table, and the completeness conditions bound their fraction.}
{若 B1、B2 在 $D_s$ 上成立，且 $C(m^r)$ 中每个条件都在 $D_s$ 上成立，则对每个期间 $p$，$m^r$ 的规范 SQL 返回的是角色日期落在 $p$ 内的事件度量值的 $\mathrm{AGG}$，每个事件恰好贡献一次；被遗漏的只有日期键或内连接键匹配不到被连接表任何行的事件，其比例受完整性条件约束。}
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}[\bt{Proof sketch}{证明概要}]
\bi{By B1 and filtered key uniqueness, $\sigma_\varphi(F)$ holds exactly one row per event. Join cardinality bounds the matches of each row in every $R_i$ by the permitted number, one for the dimension joins of $I$, so no join duplicates a row, and join completeness bounds the rows that an inner join drops for lack of a match; the date role joins each row to at most one date-dimension row and hence to at most one period. Every surviving row therefore contributes its measurement (B2) exactly once, to the period of its role date.}
{由 B1 和过滤后键唯一性，$\sigma_\varphi(F)$ 中每个事件恰有一行。连接基数把每行在各 $R_i$ 中的匹配数限制在允许值以内（$I$ 的维度连接允许值为一），故连接不会复制任何行；连接完整性约束内连接因匹配不到而排除的行数；日期角色把每行关联到至多一行日期维度、因而至多一个期间。于是每个保留下来的行把其度量值（B2）恰好贡献一次，归入其角色日期所在的期间。}
\end{proof}
\bi{The proposition also fixes the detection boundary. B2 is about values: an update that rescales a measure column violates no condition (the unit change of Section~\ref{sec:scenarios}). B1 may be satisfied by several populations at once: a primary table and an appended full backup each hold one row per key, and nothing structural tells which is the business population (Section~\ref{sec:repair-eval}). A fact whose date key is valid but wrong is assigned to a period, not detected. Conversely, every data-only update that makes the canonical SQL count an event twice, or drop facts beyond the admitted fraction, violates a condition and is caught at the next maintenance or declared use.}
{命题同时确定了检测的边界。B2 关乎取值：把度量列整体改变量纲的更新不违反任何条件（第~\ref{sec:scenarios} 节的单位变化）。B1 可能同时被多个总体满足：主表与追加的整份备份副本各自都是每键一行，结构上无从判断哪一份是业务总体（第~\ref{sec:repair-eval} 节）。日期键合法但错误的事实会被归入某个期间，而不会被发现。反过来，凡是使规范 SQL 把一个事件计数两次、或使超过准入比例的事实被排除的纯数据更新，都会违反某个条件，并在下一次维护或声明使用时被发现。}

\subsection{\bt{Versions, check results, and reuse}{版本、检查结果与复用}}
\bi{The version $V(T)$ of a table identifies the state of $T$ that a check read. \system\ supports two sources. The default combines the schema fingerprint, DML counters, and the ETL batch identifier; it is cheap but neither transactional nor immediate, because PostgreSQL reports DML statistics asynchronously after commit. \emph{Transactional versions} are maintained by a statement-level trigger that increments a per-table counter inside every writing transaction; the counter is sharded by backend process so that concurrent writers do not contend on one row, and the version is the sum of the shards. A check result is the outcome of $Q_c$ at the versions it read and is indexed by}
{表的版本 $V(T)$ 标识检查读到的 $T$ 的状态。\system\ 支持两种版本来源。默认来源组合结构指纹、DML 计数和 ETL 批次号，代价很低，但既不随事务提交也不即时可见，因为 PostgreSQL 在提交后异步上报 DML 统计。\emph{事务性版本}由语句级触发器维护：每个写事务内把对应表的计数加一；计数按后端进程分片，使并发写入者不争同一行，版本取各分片之和。检查结果是 $Q_c$ 在其所读版本上的执行结果，以如下键索引：}
\[
\bigl(\operatorname{id}(c),\,V(\operatorname{dep}(c))\bigr).
\]
\bi{A check result serves every definition that requires the same condition at the same dependency versions. The canonical form is semantic rather than textual: the order of key columns and the formatting of filters do not change it, so a grain condition and a join cardinality check that state the same key in different orders share one check result.}
{一个检查结果服务于所有在相同依赖版本上需要同一条件的定义。规范形式按语义而非文本确定：键列顺序与过滤的书写格式不影响它，因此以不同顺序写出同一键的粒度条件与连接基数检查共享一个检查结果。}

\subsection{\bt{States and the execution contract}{状态与执行契约}}
\bi{A revision is \emph{valid} when its conditions hold at the current versions of their dependencies. A dependency-version change makes it \emph{pending}, a state derived by version comparison that triggers revalidation. Revalidation either refreshes the evidence or \emph{invalidates} the revision when a required condition fails. Repair produces a new \emph{candidate} revision, which becomes valid after passing the checks in Section~\ref{sec:maintenance} and replaces the invalidated one. An optimization publishes a revision that \emph{supersedes} a valid predecessor (Section~\ref{sec:optimization}); the predecessor stays usable while its own conditions hold, and it becomes current again if its successor fails only a premise that the predecessor does not need. An invalidated revision is never accepted again, and revision numbers are never reused. For a query $q$ that declares $m^r$ and executes on snapshot $s$, the execution contract is}
{当修订的全部条件在其依赖的当前版本上成立时，修订是\emph{有效}的。依赖版本变化使其进入\emph{待验证}状态；这一状态由版本比较得出，并触发重验证。重验证要么刷新证据，要么在必需条件失败时使修订\emph{失效}。修复产生新的\emph{候选}修订，通过第~\ref{sec:maintenance} 节的检查后变为有效，并替代失效的修订。优化发布的修订\emph{取代}一个仍然有效的前一修订（第~\ref{sec:optimization} 节）：前一修订在其自身条件成立时仍可使用；若后继修订只因一条前一修订并不需要的前提而失效，前一修订重新成为当前修订。失效的修订不再被接受，修订号也从不复用。对声明使用 $m^r$、在快照 $s$ 上执行的查询 $q$，执行契约为}
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
\operatorname{Admit}(q,m^r,s)\Longrightarrow {}& \operatorname{Available}(m^r,s)\\
&{}\land\bigwedge_{c\in C(m^r)}c(D_s),
\end{split}
\end{equation}
\bi{where $D_s$ is the database at snapshot $s$. Version checks alone enforce availability but establish the conditions on the snapshot of the most recent maintenance; same-snapshot validation (Section~\ref{sec:snapshot}) establishes them on $s$ itself.}
{其中 $D_s$ 为快照 $s$ 上的数据库。单靠版本校验只能保证可用性，条件则是在最近一次维护所在的快照上成立；同快照验证（第~\ref{sec:snapshot} 节）使条件在 $s$ 本身上成立。}
