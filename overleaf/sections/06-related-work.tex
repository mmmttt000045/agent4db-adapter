\section{\bt{Related Work}{相关工作}}
\label{sec:related}
\paragraph{\bt{Agent memory}{智能体记忆}}
\bi{Agent memories retain facts and procedures~\cite{chhikara2025mem0,packer2023memgpt,wang2025awm,zhao2024expel,ouyang2026reasoningbank,zhang2026ace,xu2025amem,zhang2025apc}, share trajectories and govern access~\cite{zhang2025gmemory,fioresi2026learning,rezazadeh2025collaborative,margalit2026governed}, and manage invalidation, versions, transactions, and repair~\cite{wu2026invalidation,chen2026fresh,li2026memtx,zhao2026memorepair}. AgentSM composes recurring database tool sequences~\cite{biswal2026agentsm}. \system{} couples executable definitions with consumer-side data evidence.}
{智能体记忆保留事实和过程~\cite{chhikara2025mem0,packer2023memgpt,wang2025awm,zhao2024expel,ouyang2026reasoningbank,zhang2026ace,xu2025amem,zhang2025apc}，共享轨迹、管理访问~\cite{zhang2025gmemory,fioresi2026learning,rezazadeh2025collaborative,margalit2026governed}，并处理失效、版本、事务及修复~\cite{wu2026invalidation,chen2026fresh,li2026memtx,zhao2026memorepair}。AgentSM 组合重复的数据库工具序列~\cite{biswal2026agentsm}；\system{} 将可执行定义与消费者侧数据证据关联。}
\paragraph{\bt{Data agents and semantics}{数据智能体与语义}}
\bi{Database knowledge and business semantics support data agents~\cite{liu2026agents,luo2026dataagents,lei2025spider2,floratou2024nl2sql,li2023bird,sequeda2024kg,huo2026birdinteract,jiang2025siriusbi}. DataLab extracts script logic and updates notebook dependencies~\cite{weng2025datalab}; MetricFlow and Databricks centralize metrics~\cite{dbt2026metricflow,databricks2026metricviews}, with MetricFlow configuration/executability checks~\cite{dbt2026metricflowvalidation}. \system{} adds learned, snapshot-specific grain/join evidence (Table~\ref{tab:capabilities}). RelBench supports relational learning~\cite{robinson2024relbench,fey2024rdl}; feature-engineering systems~\cite{hollmann2023caafe,guo2024dsagent,liang2025featpilot,gao2024ctxpipe,fan2026deepprep,ge2025knowtrans} construct features; \system{} reuses them with per-instance time bounds and consumer audits.}
{数据库知识和业务语义支撑数据智能体~\cite{liu2026agents,luo2026dataagents,lei2025spider2,floratou2024nl2sql,li2023bird,sequeda2024kg,huo2026birdinteract,jiang2025siriusbi}。DataLab 提取脚本逻辑并更新笔记本依赖~\cite{weng2025datalab}，MetricFlow 与 Databricks 集中管理指标~\cite{dbt2026metricflow,databricks2026metricviews}，MetricFlow 提供配置／可执行性检查~\cite{dbt2026metricflowvalidation}；\system{} 增加学习得到的快照级粒度／连接证据（表~\ref{tab:capabilities}）。RelBench 支撑关系学习~\cite{robinson2024relbench,fey2024rdl}；特征工程系统~\cite{hollmann2023caafe,guo2024dsagent,liang2025featpilot,gao2024ctxpipe,fan2026deepprep,ge2025knowtrans}构建特征，\system{} 通过逐实例时间界限和消费者审计复用特征。}
\paragraph{\bt{Database work sharing}{数据库工作共享}}
\bi{Query sharing~\cite{sellis1988mqo,harizopoulos2005qpipe,giannikis2012shareddb}, result/semantic caching~\cite{ivanova2010recycling,dar1996semantic}, invalidation and dependency keys~\cite{garrod2008ferdinand,gunda2010nectar}, leases and transactional caches~\cite{nishtala2013memcache,ports2010txcache}, and incremental/deferred/lazy maintenance~\cite{gupta1995maintenance,blakeley1986efficiently,colby1996deferred,zhou2007lazy} preserve computation. Dependency traces~\cite{mokhov2018build,hammer2014adapton}, summarizability~\cite{lenz1997summarizability,mazon2009summarizability,horner2004additivity}, integrity checks~\cite{nicolas1982integrity,ceri1990constraint}, and validation tools~\cite{schelter2018deequ,breck2019validation,dbt2026tests} support maintenance and validation. \system{} derives conditions from learned definitions and shares versioned evidence.}
{查询共享~\cite{sellis1988mqo,harizopoulos2005qpipe,giannikis2012shareddb}、结果／语义缓存~\cite{ivanova2010recycling,dar1996semantic}、失效与依赖键~\cite{garrod2008ferdinand,gunda2010nectar}、租约和事务缓存~\cite{nishtala2013memcache,ports2010txcache}以及增量／延迟／惰性维护~\cite{gupta1995maintenance,blakeley1986efficiently,colby1996deferred,zhou2007lazy}保留计算。依赖轨迹~\cite{mokhov2018build,hammer2014adapton}、汇总性~\cite{lenz1997summarizability,mazon2009summarizability,horner2004additivity}、完整性检查~\cite{nicolas1982integrity,ceri1990constraint}和验证工具~\cite{schelter2018deequ,breck2019validation,dbt2026tests}支撑维护与验证；\system{} 从学得定义派生条件、共享版本化证据。}
