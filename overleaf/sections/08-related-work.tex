\section{\bt{Related Work}{相关工作}}
\label{sec:related}
\paragraph{\bt{Agent memory}{智能体记忆}}
\bi{Memory systems for LLM agents keep one agent's experience: long-term facts and conversations~\cite{chhikara2025mem0,packer2023memgpt}, workflows induced from successful trajectories~\cite{wang2025awm}, insights and strategies~\cite{zhao2024expel,ouyang2026reasoningbank}, evolving context playbooks~\cite{zhang2026ace}, linked notes~\cite{xu2025amem}, plan templates~\cite{zhang2025apc}, and past solutions~\cite{guo2024dsagent}; a tutorial treats agentic memory as a data-management problem~\cite{li2026agenticmemory}. Their knowledge concerns the agent's own tasks, evolves by the agent's own feedback, and is learned again by every agent. Sharing between agents has so far appeared within one task: Learning to Share lets the parallel agents of one task add selected intermediate steps to a common memory to save computation~\cite{fioresi2026learning}. For memories that several agents read, recent work keeps derived records consistent through version stamps or explicit revocation: invalidation contracts evict cached fixes after data drift~\cite{wu2026invalidation}, PlanFence checks that a shared plan was derived from current input versions~\cite{chen2026fresh}, MemTX commits writes through snapshot-isolated transactions~\cite{li2026memtx}, and \textsc{MemoRepair} republishes the descendants of a corrected source after validation~\cite{zhao2026memorepair}. \system\ shares across independent agents and sessions, and its records describe a database that changes underneath them; it checks them against that database with conditions derived from each record, repairs or invalidates them once for every agent, and binds their use to the snapshot a query reads.}
{大模型智能体的记忆系统保存一个智能体的经验：长期事实与对话~\cite{chhikara2025mem0,packer2023memgpt}，从成功轨迹中归纳的工作流~\cite{wang2025awm}，洞见与策略~\cite{zhao2024expel,ouyang2026reasoningbank}，不断演化的上下文手册~\cite{zhang2026ace}，互相链接的笔记~\cite{xu2025amem}，计划模板~\cite{zhang2025apc}，以及既往方案~\cite{guo2024dsagent}；一篇教程把智能体记忆当作数据管理问题来讨论~\cite{li2026agenticmemory}。这些知识关于智能体自己的任务，依据智能体自己的反馈演化，每个智能体都要重新学一遍。智能体之间的共享目前出现在同一任务之内：Learning to Share 让同一任务的并行智能体把选出的中间步骤加入公共记忆，以节省计算~\cite{fioresi2026learning}。对于多个智能体读取的记忆，近期工作借助版本戳或显式撤销保持派生记录一致：失效契约在数据漂移后驱逐缓存的修正~\cite{wu2026invalidation}，PlanFence 检查共享计划是否由输入的当前版本推出~\cite{chen2026fresh}，MemTX 通过快照隔离的事务提交写入~\cite{li2026memtx}，\textsc{MemoRepair} 在验证后重新发布被更正来源的派生条目~\cite{zhao2026memorepair}。\system\ 在相互独立的智能体与会话之间共享，其记录描述一个会在其下变化的数据库；它用从每条记录推出的条件对照该数据库检查记录，为所有智能体一次性地修复或使其失效，并把使用绑定到查询所读的快照。}

\paragraph{\bt{Data agents and business semantics}{数据智能体与业务语义}}
\bi{Agent-first data systems observe that agent requests are highly redundant and call for a persistent memory that serves many agents~\cite{liu2026agents}; a tutorial surveys data agents and their difficulty with changing data~\cite{luo2026dataagents}. Enterprise schemas remain hard for text-to-SQL~\cite{lei2025spider2,floratou2024nl2sql}, explicit business semantics raise accuracy~\cite{li2023bird,sequeda2024kg}, and interactive benchmarks treat missing business knowledge as a failure to recover from~\cite{huo2026birdinteract}. Business-intelligence platforms maintain term definitions~\cite{jiang2025siriusbi} or generate calculation logic from script history~\cite{weng2025datalab}, metric layers let engineers declare definitions by hand~\cite{dbt2026metricflow,databricks2026metricviews}, and AgentSM organizes a text-to-SQL system's past trajectories as semantic memory, leaving shared memory to future work~\cite{biswal2026agentsm}. \system\ learns such definitions from agents' sessions and shares them; a text-to-SQL agent is a consumer of the memory, not a substitute for it. Robustness to schema variation~\cite{zhang2025evoschema,furst2025datamodel} is complementary.}
{面向智能体的数据系统指出智能体请求高度重复，并呼吁为众多智能体服务的持久记忆~\cite{liu2026agents}；一篇教程综述了数据智能体及其难以适应变化数据的问题~\cite{luo2026dataagents}。企业表结构对 Text-to-SQL 仍然困难~\cite{lei2025spider2,floratou2024nl2sql}，显式业务语义能提高正确率~\cite{li2023bird,sequeda2024kg}，交互式基准把缺失的业务知识当作需要弥补的失败~\cite{huo2026birdinteract}。商业智能平台维护术语定义~\cite{jiang2025siriusbi}或从脚本历史生成计算逻辑~\cite{weng2025datalab}，指标层由工程师人工声明定义~\cite{dbt2026metricflow,databricks2026metricviews}，AgentSM 把一个 Text-to-SQL 系统过去的轨迹组织为语义记忆，并把共享记忆留作未来工作~\cite{biswal2026agentsm}。\system\ 从智能体的会话中学到这些定义并加以共享；Text-to-SQL 智能体是记忆的使用者，而不是它的替代。表结构变化下的鲁棒性~\cite{zhang2025evoschema,furst2025datamodel}与本文互补。}

\paragraph{\bt{Sharing work in database systems}{数据库系统中的工作共享}}
\bi{Databases have long avoided repeated work: multi-query optimization and shared execution evaluate the common work of concurrent queries once~\cite{sellis1988mqo,harizopoulos2005qpipe,giannikis2012shareddb}, recycling keeps intermediate results and invalidates those affected by updates~\cite{ivanova2010recycling}, and caches reuse results through semantic descriptions~\cite{dar1996semantic}, update-driven invalidation~\cite{garrod2008ferdinand}, input-dependent keys~\cite{gunda2010nectar}, leases that merge concurrent misses~\cite{nishtala2013memcache}, and transactional consistency~\cite{ports2010txcache}. Materialized views are maintained incrementally~\cite{gupta1995maintenance,blakeley1986efficiently}, deferred off the update path~\cite{colby1996deferred}, or refreshed when referenced~\cite{zhou2007lazy}, and build systems verify dependency traces~\cite{mokhov2018build,hammer2014adapton}. These techniques share the results of queries, which repeat in applications~\cite{ma2018querybot}; agents repeat knowledge instead, and their SQL rarely repeats (Table~\ref{tab:workload-characterization}). \system\ shares the knowledge behind the queries, and it reuses check results under a reuse rule proved sound (Lemma~\ref{lem:reuse}).}
{数据库一向避免重复工作：多查询优化与共享执行对并发查询的公共工作只计算一次~\cite{sellis1988mqo,harizopoulos2005qpipe,giannikis2012shareddb}，中间结果回收保留中间结果并使受更新影响的结果失效~\cite{ivanova2010recycling}，缓存借助语义描述~\cite{dar1996semantic}、更新驱动失效~\cite{garrod2008ferdinand}、依赖输入的键~\cite{gunda2010nectar}、合并并发未命中的租约~\cite{nishtala2013memcache}和事务一致性~\cite{ports2010txcache}复用结果。物化视图可以增量维护~\cite{gupta1995maintenance,blakeley1986efficiently}、移出更新路径~\cite{colby1996deferred}或在被引用时刷新~\cite{zhou2007lazy}，构建系统核验依赖轨迹~\cite{mokhov2018build,hammer2014adapton}。这些技术共享的是查询的结果，而查询在应用中会重复~\cite{ma2018querybot}；智能体重复的则是知识，它们的 SQL 很少重复（表~\ref{tab:workload-characterization}）。\system\ 共享查询背后的知识，并以被证明可靠的复用规则复用检查结果（引理~\ref{lem:reuse}）。}

\paragraph{\bt{Constraints and data validation}{约束与数据验证}}
\bi{Summarizability research identifies disjointness, completeness, and type compatibility as conditions for correct aggregation~\cite{lenz1997summarizability,mazon2009summarizability,horner2004additivity}, and measures in SQL attach aggregation semantics to tables~\cite{hyde2024measures}. Integrity checking specializes verification to the updates that can violate a constraint~\cite{nicolas1982integrity,ceri1990constraint,blakeley1989irrelevant}; soft constraints hold on current data without enforcement~\cite{godfrey2001soft,ilyas2004cords}, and profiling discovers and maintains unique column combinations and dependencies~\cite{abedjan2015profiling,abedjan2014swan,schirmer2019dynfd}. Data-validation systems check declared or inferred constraints on growing data~\cite{schelter2018deequ,breck2019validation,song2021autovalidate,redyuk2021dynamic}, and pipeline tooling runs key and relationship tests~\cite{dbt2026tests}, expectation suites~\cite{gx2026overview}, and data contracts~\cite{bitol2026odcs}. These checks are declared on tables, and a failure stops a build without saying which consumer depended on it. \system\ derives its checks from each shared definition, proves them sufficient for its aggregate, and lets their outcomes decide what every agent may use.}
{可汇总性研究指出，不相交、完备和类型相容是正确聚合的条件~\cite{lenz1997summarizability,mazon2009summarizability,horner2004additivity}，SQL 中的度量把聚合语义附着在表上~\cite{hyde2024measures}。完整性检查按可能违反约束的更新执行验证~\cite{nicolas1982integrity,ceri1990constraint,blakeley1989irrelevant}；软约束在当前数据上成立但不被强制~\cite{godfrey2001soft,ilyas2004cords}，数据画像发现并维护唯一列组合与依赖~\cite{abedjan2015profiling,abedjan2014swan,schirmer2019dynfd}。数据验证系统在增长的数据上检查声明或推断的约束~\cite{schelter2018deequ,breck2019validation,song2021autovalidate,redyuk2021dynamic}，数据管道工具执行键与关系测试~\cite{dbt2026tests}、期望集~\cite{gx2026overview}和数据契约~\cite{bitol2026odcs}。这些检查声明在表上，失败时中止构建，却不说明哪个使用方依赖它。\system\ 的检查由每个共享定义推出，证明对其聚合是充分的，并由检查结果决定每个智能体可以使用什么。}
