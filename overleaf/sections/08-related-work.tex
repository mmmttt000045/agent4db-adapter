\section{\bt{Related Work}{相关工作}}
\label{sec:related}
\paragraph{\bt{Agent memory}{智能体记忆}}
\bi{Agent memories retain facts and conversations~\cite{chhikara2025mem0,packer2023memgpt}, workflows~\cite{wang2025awm}, strategies~\cite{zhao2024expel,ouyang2026reasoningbank}, playbooks~\cite{zhang2026ace}, linked notes~\cite{xu2025amem}, and plans~\cite{zhang2025apc}. G-Memory shares hierarchical trajectories and insights within a multi-agent system~\cite{zhang2025gmemory}; Learning to Share reuses intermediate steps within a task~\cite{fioresi2026learning}, while Collaborative Memory and MemClaw govern access and writes across users or agents~\cite{rezazadeh2025collaborative,margalit2026governed}. Recent systems also support invalidation, version checks, transactions, and repair~\cite{wu2026invalidation,chen2026fresh,li2026memtx,zhao2026memorepair}. \system\ connects these concerns to executable database definitions: it derives data conditions from a definition, shares their validation by dependency version, and checks them on the consuming query's snapshot.}
{智能体记忆保存事实与对话~\cite{chhikara2025mem0,packer2023memgpt}、工作流~\cite{wang2025awm}、策略~\cite{zhao2024expel,ouyang2026reasoningbank}、手册~\cite{zhang2026ace}、相互链接的笔记~\cite{xu2025amem}和计划~\cite{zhang2025apc}。G-Memory 在多智能体系统内共享分层轨迹与洞见~\cite{zhang2025gmemory}；Learning to Share 在任务内复用中间步骤~\cite{fioresi2026learning}，Collaborative Memory 与 MemClaw 则管理跨用户或智能体的访问和写入~\cite{rezazadeh2025collaborative,margalit2026governed}。近期系统还支持失效、版本检查、事务和修复~\cite{wu2026invalidation,chen2026fresh,li2026memtx,zhao2026memorepair}。\system\ 将这些问题与可执行数据库定义联系起来：从定义派生数据条件，按依赖版本共享验证，并在消费查询的快照上检查这些条件。}

\paragraph{\bt{Database exploration memory}{数据库探索记忆}}
\bi{AgentSM is a close comparison for metric-query workloads~\cite{biswal2026agentsm}. It structures prior or synthesized trajectories, retrieves the most similar question within the same database, and constructs composite tools from recurring tool sequences. These mechanisms directly reduce repeated exploration. Its described reuse path does not derive and maintain metric-grain and join conditions on the consuming query's snapshot. \system\ makes those conditions part of each published definition and repairs or revokes failed revisions. Rather than retrieving raw trajectories or tool sequences, \system\ compiles parameterized metric and feature definitions and maintains their grain and join conditions on the consuming query's snapshot.}
{AgentSM 是指标查询负载上的直接比较对象~\cite{biswal2026agentsm}。它将既往或合成轨迹结构化，在同一数据库内检索最相似的问题，并从反复出现的工具序列构造复合工具。这些机制直接减少重复探索。其所述复用途径未在消费查询的快照上推导并维护指标粒度与连接条件。\system\ 将这些条件纳入每个发布的定义，并修复或撤销失效版本。\system\ 将参数化的指标与特征定义编译为查询，并在消费查询的快照上维护其粒度与连接条件，而不只是检索原始轨迹或工具序列。}

\paragraph{\bt{Data agents and business semantics}{数据智能体与业务语义}}
\bi{Agent-first data systems motivate persistent database knowledge~\cite{liu2026agents,luo2026dataagents}; enterprise text-to-SQL and BI systems benefit from explicit business semantics~\cite{lei2025spider2,floratou2024nl2sql,li2023bird,sequeda2024kg,huo2026birdinteract,jiang2025siriusbi}. DataLab already extracts metadata and calculation logic from historical SQL/Python scripts, organizes knowledge for retrieval, and translates requests through a DSL for downstream generation~\cite{weng2025datalab}. It also updates notebook dependency graphs after edits. \system's contribution is the lifecycle of executable definitions: publication from independently verified sessions, derived data conditions, shared checks bound to query snapshots, and repair or revocation after condition failure.}
{面向智能体的数据系统提出持久数据库知识的需求~\cite{liu2026agents,luo2026dataagents}；企业 Text-to-SQL 与 BI 系统受益于明确的业务语义~\cite{lei2025spider2,floratou2024nl2sql,li2023bird,sequeda2024kg,huo2026birdinteract,jiang2025siriusbi}。DataLab 已从历史 SQL/Python 脚本提取元数据与计算逻辑，组织知识以支持检索，并通过 DSL 将请求转交后续生成流程~\cite{weng2025datalab}；它还在编辑后更新笔记本依赖图。\system\ 的贡献是可执行定义的生命周期：从独立核实的会话发布定义，派生数据条件，将共享检查绑定查询快照，并在条件失效后修复或撤销。}

\bi{MetricFlow and Databricks metric views centralize definitions and query generation~\cite{dbt2026metricflow,databricks2026metricviews}, so definition management already benefits multiple consumers. The key architectural distinction is automatic maintenance of derived data conditions: Databricks explicitly documents that its \code{at\_most\_one\_match} declaration is not validated at runtime. \system\ checks the modeled multiplicity and coverage conditions on the query snapshot, subject to the business assumptions in Proposition~\ref{prop:sufficiency}.}
{MetricFlow 与 Databricks 指标视图集中管理定义与查询生成~\cite{dbt2026metricflow,databricks2026metricviews}，因此定义管理已经让多个消费者受益。核心架构区别是自动维护从定义派生的数据条件：Databricks 明确说明其 \code{at\_most\_one\_match} 声明不在运行时验证。\system\ 在查询快照上检查已建模的基数与覆盖条件，其保证以命题~\ref{prop:sufficiency} 中的业务假设为前提。}

\bi{\emph{Prediction over relational data.} RelBench poses forecasting tasks over multi-table databases~\cite{robinson2024relbench} and is the testbed of relational deep learning~\cite{fey2024rdl}; LLM agents engineer features for such tasks by writing and validating code or SQL~\cite{hollmann2023caafe,guo2024dsagent}. The database community automates data preparation for learning: FeatPilot searches join paths for the features that most improve a model~\cite{liang2025featpilot}, CtxPipe builds preparation pipelines for one table by reinforcement learning~\cite{gao2024ctxpipe}, DeepPrep explores operator pipelines with an LLM agent over a search tree~\cite{fan2026deepprep}, and KnowTrans transfers knowledge between data-preparation tasks by fine-tuning~\cite{ge2025knowtrans}. These works prepare data for one task at a time; \system\ makes the features that one agent verified reusable by every later session and every other task on the same database, and compiles them so that they cannot read data after the seed time.}
{\emph{关系数据上的预测。} RelBench 在多表数据库上提出预测任务~\cite{robinson2024relbench}，也是关系深度学习的实验平台~\cite{fey2024rdl}；LLM 智能体通过编写并验证代码或 SQL 为这类任务构建特征~\cite{hollmann2023caafe,guo2024dsagent}。数据库领域为学习任务自动做数据准备：FeatPilot 搜索对模型提升最大的连接路径特征~\cite{liang2025featpilot}，CtxPipe 用强化学习为单张表构建准备流水线~\cite{gao2024ctxpipe}，DeepPrep 用 LLM 智能体在搜索树上探索算子流水线~\cite{fan2026deepprep}，KnowTrans 通过微调在数据准备任务之间迁移知识~\cite{ge2025knowtrans}。这些工作一次为一个任务准备数据；\system\ 让一个智能体验证过的特征可被同一数据库上之后的每个会话和其他每个任务复用，并把它们编译成不可能读到种子时间之后的数据。}

\paragraph{\bt{Sharing work in database systems}{数据库系统中的工作共享}}
\bi{Databases have long avoided repeated work: multi-query optimization and shared execution evaluate the common work of concurrent queries once~\cite{sellis1988mqo,harizopoulos2005qpipe,giannikis2012shareddb}, recycling keeps intermediate results and invalidates those affected by updates~\cite{ivanova2010recycling}, and caches reuse results through semantic descriptions~\cite{dar1996semantic}, update-driven invalidation~\cite{garrod2008ferdinand}, input-dependent keys~\cite{gunda2010nectar}, leases that merge concurrent misses~\cite{nishtala2013memcache}, and transactional consistency~\cite{ports2010txcache}. Materialized views are maintained incrementally~\cite{gupta1995maintenance,blakeley1986efficiently}, deferred off the update path~\cite{colby1996deferred}, or refreshed when referenced~\cite{zhou2007lazy}, and build systems verify dependency traces~\cite{mokhov2018build,hammer2014adapton}. Semantic caching supports reuse across different queries, and TxCache already binds cached values to a consistent database snapshot. \system\ applies dependency tracking and snapshot consistency to validation evidence for learned definitions (Lemma~\ref{lem:reuse}).}
{数据库一向避免重复工作：多查询优化与共享执行对并发查询的公共工作只计算一次~\cite{sellis1988mqo,harizopoulos2005qpipe,giannikis2012shareddb}，中间结果回收保留中间结果并使受更新影响的结果失效~\cite{ivanova2010recycling}，缓存借助语义描述~\cite{dar1996semantic}、更新驱动失效~\cite{garrod2008ferdinand}、依赖输入的键~\cite{gunda2010nectar}、合并并发未命中的租约~\cite{nishtala2013memcache}和事务一致性~\cite{ports2010txcache}复用结果。物化视图可以增量维护~\cite{gupta1995maintenance,blakeley1986efficiently}、移出更新路径~\cite{colby1996deferred}或在被引用时刷新~\cite{zhou2007lazy}，构建系统核验依赖轨迹~\cite{mokhov2018build,hammer2014adapton}。语义缓存支持不同查询之间的复用，TxCache 已将缓存值绑定到一致的数据库快照。\system\ 将依赖跟踪与快照一致性用于学得定义的验证证据（引理~\ref{lem:reuse}）。}

\paragraph{\bt{Constraints and data validation}{约束与数据验证}}
\bi{Summarizability research identifies disjointness and completeness as conditions for correct aggregation~\cite{lenz1997summarizability,mazon2009summarizability,horner2004additivity}, integrity checking specializes verification to the updates that can violate a constraint~\cite{nicolas1982integrity,ceri1990constraint}, and data-validation systems and pipeline tests check declared constraints on tables~\cite{schelter2018deequ,breck2019validation,dbt2026tests}. Building on foundational summarizability and integrity-checking primitives, \system\ automatically derives these conditions from learned definitions and integrates their shared verification into query admission and repair.}
{汇总性研究指出不相交与完整是聚合正确的条件~\cite{lenz1997summarizability,mazon2009summarizability,horner2004additivity}，完整性检查把验证限定在可能违反约束的更新上~\cite{nicolas1982integrity,ceri1990constraint}，数据验证系统与管道测试在表上检查声明的约束~\cite{schelter2018deequ,breck2019validation,dbt2026tests}。基于汇总性与完整性检查的基础机制，\system\ 自动从学得的定义推导这些条件，并将条件的共享验证纳入查询准入和修复。}
