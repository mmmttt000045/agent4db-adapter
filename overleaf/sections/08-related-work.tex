\section{\bt{Related Work}{相关工作}}
\label{sec:related}
\paragraph{\bt{Data agents and business semantics}{数据智能体与业务语义}}
\bi{Agent-first data systems identify redundant agent requests and propose a persistent agentic memory that must track changes to data and metadata~\cite{liu2026agents}; tutorials survey data agents and their difficulty with changing data~\cite{luo2026dataagents} and the management of agentic memory~\cite{li2026agenticmemory}. Enterprise schemas and terminology remain hard for text-to-SQL systems~\cite{lei2025spider2,floratou2024nl2sql}, and explicit business semantics raise accuracy~\cite{li2023bird,huo2026birdinteract,sequeda2024kg}. Business-intelligence platforms maintain term definitions~\cite{jiang2025siriusbi} or generate calculation logic from script history and lineage~\cite{weng2025datalab}; AgentSM organizes trajectories as structured semantic memory and leaves the consistency of shared memory to future work~\cite{biswal2026agentsm}. \system\ provides that consistency for metric definitions: executable conditions connect a definition's recorded meaning and implementation to current database state, and their verdicts decide whether and on which snapshot the definition may be used. Schema and data-model robustness studies~\cite{zhang2025evoschema,furst2025datamodel} address complementary variation; our evaluation isolates data-only breakage under a fixed schema.}
{面向智能体的数据系统指出智能体请求高度重复，并提出必须跟踪数据与元数据变化的持久智能体记忆~\cite{liu2026agents}；教程综述了数据智能体及其难以适应变化数据的问题~\cite{luo2026dataagents}和智能体记忆的管理~\cite{li2026agenticmemory}。企业表结构与术语对 Text-to-SQL 系统仍然困难~\cite{lei2025spider2,floratou2024nl2sql}，显式业务语义能够提高正确率~\cite{li2023bird,huo2026birdinteract,sequeda2024kg}。商业智能平台维护术语定义~\cite{jiang2025siriusbi}，或从脚本历史和血缘中生成计算逻辑~\cite{weng2025datalab}；AgentSM 把轨迹组织为结构化语义记忆，并把共享记忆的一致性留作未来工作~\cite{biswal2026agentsm}。\system\ 为指标定义提供这种一致性：可执行条件把定义记录的含义与实现联系到当前数据库状态，其结论决定定义能否使用、在哪个快照上使用。表结构与数据模型鲁棒性研究~\cite{zhang2025evoschema,furst2025datamodel}处理互补的差异；本文评估单独考察固定表结构下的纯数据破坏。}

\paragraph{\bt{Agent memory and experience reuse}{智能体记忆与经验复用}}
\bi{Agent memory systems induce workflows from successful trajectories~\cite{wang2025awm}, distill insights or strategies from experience~\cite{zhao2024expel,ouyang2026reasoningbank}, evolve context playbooks~\cite{zhang2026ace}, link and update notes~\cite{xu2025amem}, manage long-term facts~\cite{chhikara2025mem0,packer2023memgpt}, cache plan templates~\cite{zhang2025apc}, and store and reuse past solutions~\cite{guo2024dsagent}. These representations organize knowledge across tasks and conversations. \system\ represents shared metric knowledge together with typed database conditions and versioned evidence; condition outcomes govern admission and every later use, and a failed condition leads to a bounded, regression-tested repair or to withdrawal.}
{智能体记忆系统从成功轨迹中归纳工作流~\cite{wang2025awm}，从经验中提炼洞见或策略~\cite{zhao2024expel,ouyang2026reasoningbank}，演化上下文手册~\cite{zhang2026ace}，链接并更新笔记~\cite{xu2025amem}，管理长期事实~\cite{chhikara2025mem0,packer2023memgpt}，缓存计划模板~\cite{zhang2025apc}，并保存和复用既往方案~\cite{guo2024dsagent}。这些表示在任务与对话之间组织知识。\system\ 把共享指标知识与类型化数据库条件、版本化证据一起表示；条件结论决定准入与之后的每一次使用，条件失败则导致有界的、经过回归测试的修复或撤下定义。}

\paragraph{\bt{Metric layers and summarizability}{指标层与可汇总性}}
\bi{Dimensional modeling conforms dimensions and facts across business processes~\cite{kimball2013toolkit}, and measures in SQL attach aggregation semantics to tables so that joins do not double count~\cite{hyde2024measures}. Summarizability research identifies disjointness, completeness, and type compatibility as necessary conditions for correct aggregation~\cite{lenz1997summarizability}, surveys how complex multidimensional structures violate them~\cite{mazon2009summarizability}, and proposes summary constraints analogous to integrity constraints~\cite{horner2004additivity}. Metric layers declare keys, join properties, and measures by hand: dbt's semantic layer compiles metrics from semantic models of entities, measures, and dimensions~\cite{dbt2026metricflow}, and at least one commercial layer documents that a declared join property is not validated at runtime~\cite{databricks2026metricviews}. \system\ turns these conditions into executable checks attached to learned definitions, proves them sufficient for the definition's aggregate, and enforces them where the definitions are used.}
{维度建模要求在各业务过程间使用一致的维度和事实~\cite{kimball2013toolkit}；SQL 中的度量把聚合语义附着在表上，使连接不再重复计数~\cite{hyde2024measures}。可汇总性研究指出，不相交、完备和类型相容是正确汇总的必要条件~\cite{lenz1997summarizability}，综述了复杂多维结构违反这些条件的方式~\cite{mazon2009summarizability}，并提出类似完整性约束的汇总约束~\cite{horner2004additivity}。指标层由人工声明键、连接性质和度量：dbt 的语义层从由实体、度量和维度组成的语义模型编译指标~\cite{dbt2026metricflow}，至少有一家商用指标层在文档中写明声明的连接性质不在运行时验证~\cite{databricks2026metricviews}。\system\ 把这些条件变成附着在学到的定义上的可执行检查，证明它们对定义的聚合是充分的，并在定义被使用处强制它们。}

\paragraph{\bt{Constraints, profiling, and data validation}{约束、数据画像与数据验证}}
\bi{Integrity checking specializes verification to updates that can violate a constraint~\cite{nicolas1982integrity,ceri1990constraint}, and derived-relation maintenance can first identify irrelevant updates~\cite{blakeley1989irrelevant}. Soft constraints hold on current data without being enforced and support optimization while valid~\cite{godfrey2001soft}; CORDS discovers approximate dependencies from samples~\cite{ilyas2004cords}. Profiling discovers and maintains unique column combinations and dependencies~\cite{abedjan2015profiling,abedjan2014swan,schirmer2019dynfd}. Data-validation systems check declarative constraints on growing data and share their scans~\cite{schelter2018deequ}, and infer constraints~\cite{breck2019validation,song2021autovalidate} or acceptable batch profiles~\cite{redyuk2021dynamic} from past data. Pipeline tooling runs such checks in production: dbt attaches \code{unique}, \code{not\_null}, and \code{relationships} tests to models and columns and fails a test that returns rows~\cite{dbt2026tests}; Great Expectations validates an expectation suite against each batch~\cite{gx2026overview}; data contracts let producers publish the schema and quality rules that consumers may rely on~\cite{bitol2026odcs}. These checks are declared by hand on tables, and a failure stops a build or raises an alert without saying which consumer depended on it. \system\ derives its checks from each learned definition, including the definition's own filters, and lets their verdicts govern the definition rather than the table, on the snapshot the agent reads.}
{完整性检查按可能违反约束的更新执行验证~\cite{nicolas1982integrity,ceri1990constraint}，派生关系维护可以先识别无关更新~\cite{blakeley1989irrelevant}。软约束在当前数据上成立但不被强制，并在有效时支持优化~\cite{godfrey2001soft}；CORDS 从样本中发现近似依赖~\cite{ilyas2004cords}。数据画像发现并维护唯一列组合与依赖~\cite{abedjan2015profiling,abedjan2014swan,schirmer2019dynfd}。数据验证系统在增长的数据上检查声明式约束并共享扫描~\cite{schelter2018deequ}，从历史数据中推断约束~\cite{breck2019validation,song2021autovalidate}或可接受的批次画像~\cite{redyuk2021dynamic}。数据管道工具在生产环境中运行这类检查：dbt 把 \code{unique}、\code{not\_null} 和 \code{relationships} 测试附着在模型和列上，返回行的测试即告失败~\cite{dbt2026tests}；Great Expectations 对每个批次验证一组期望~\cite{gx2026overview}；数据契约让生产方发布消费方可以依赖的表结构与质量规则~\cite{bitol2026odcs}。这些检查由人工声明在表上，失败时中止构建或发出告警，却不说明哪个消费方依赖它。\system\ 的检查由每个学到的定义推出，包含定义自己的过滤，其结论约束的是定义而不是表，并且在智能体读取的快照上生效。}

\paragraph{\bt{View maintenance, shared work, and caching}{视图维护、共享计算与缓存}}
\bi{Materialized-view maintenance~\cite{gupta1995maintenance,blakeley1986efficiently} can defer work off the update path~\cite{colby1996deferred} or until a view is referenced~\cite{zhou2007lazy}; our definition-level baseline applies this lazy strategy. Multiple-query optimization and shared execution evaluate common work once~\cite{sellis1988mqo,harizopoulos2005qpipe,giannikis2012shareddb}, and recycling reuses intermediate results and invalidates those affected by updates~\cite{ivanova2010recycling}. Cache designs use semantic descriptions~\cite{dar1996semantic}, update-driven invalidation~\cite{garrod2008ferdinand}, input-dependent keys~\cite{gunda2010nectar}, and leases that merge concurrent misses~\cite{nishtala2013memcache}; TxCache keeps cached results transactionally consistent with the database through validity intervals~\cite{ports2010txcache}. Build systems track dependency hashes~\cite{mokhov2018build}, and demand-driven incremental computation recomputes requested dependencies~\cite{hammer2014adapton}. \system\ builds on these techniques: verdicts are keyed like build-system dependencies, concurrent checks merge like leased cache misses, and snapshot-bound execution applies TxCache's principle to validity verdicts, with a reuse rule proved sound for transactional versions. \system\ adds the checks themselves, derived from learned definitions, and the rule that their verdicts govern which definitions agents may use and on which snapshot.}
{物化视图维护~\cite{gupta1995maintenance,blakeley1986efficiently}可以把工作移出更新路径~\cite{colby1996deferred}，或推迟到视图被引用时~\cite{zhou2007lazy}；本文定义级基线采用这一懒维护策略。多查询优化与共享执行对公共工作只计算一次~\cite{sellis1988mqo,harizopoulos2005qpipe,giannikis2012shareddb}，中间结果回收复用中间结果，并使受更新影响的结果失效~\cite{ivanova2010recycling}。缓存设计使用语义描述~\cite{dar1996semantic}、更新驱动失效~\cite{garrod2008ferdinand}、依赖输入的键~\cite{gunda2010nectar}及合并并发未命中的租约~\cite{nishtala2013memcache}；TxCache 用有效区间使缓存结果与数据库保持事务一致~\cite{ports2010txcache}。构建系统跟踪依赖哈希~\cite{mokhov2018build}，按需增量计算重算被请求的依赖~\cite{hammer2014adapton}。\system\ 建立在这些技术之上：结论像构建系统的依赖一样按版本索引，并发的检查像带租约的缓存未命中一样合并，绑定快照的执行把 TxCache 的原则用于有效性结论，其复用规则在事务性版本下被证明可靠。\system\ 增加的是检查本身——由学到的定义推出——以及用检查结论决定智能体可以使用哪些定义、在哪个快照上使用的规则。}
