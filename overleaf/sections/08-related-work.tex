\section{\bt{Related Work}{相关工作}}
\label{sec:related}
\paragraph{\bt{Agent and exploration memory}{智能体与探索记忆}}
\bi{Agent memories retain facts/dialogue~\cite{chhikara2025mem0,packer2023memgpt}, workflows~\cite{wang2025awm}, strategies~\cite{zhao2024expel,ouyang2026reasoningbank}, playbooks~\cite{zhang2026ace}, notes~\cite{xu2025amem}, and plans~\cite{zhang2025apc}. They also share trajectories~\cite{zhang2025gmemory,fioresi2026learning}, govern access/writes~\cite{rezazadeh2025collaborative,margalit2026governed}, and address invalidation, versions, transactions, and repair~\cite{wu2026invalidation,chen2026fresh,li2026memtx,zhao2026memorepair}. AgentSM retrieves database trajectories and composes recurring tool sequences~\cite{biswal2026agentsm}. \system\ connects executable definitions to consumer-side data evidence.}
{智能体记忆保留事实／对话~\cite{chhikara2025mem0,packer2023memgpt}、工作流~\cite{wang2025awm}、策略~\cite{zhao2024expel,ouyang2026reasoningbank}、手册~\cite{zhang2026ace}、笔记~\cite{xu2025amem}与计划~\cite{zhang2025apc}；这些系统还共享轨迹~\cite{zhang2025gmemory,fioresi2026learning}、管理访问写入~\cite{rezazadeh2025collaborative,margalit2026governed}，处理失效、版本、事务与修复~\cite{wu2026invalidation,chen2026fresh,li2026memtx,zhao2026memorepair}。AgentSM检索数据库轨迹并从重复序列构造复合工具~\cite{biswal2026agentsm}。\system{}将可执行定义关联到消费者侧的数据证据。}
\paragraph{\bt{Data agents and business semantics}{数据智能体与业务语义}}
\bi{Database knowledge~\cite{liu2026agents,luo2026dataagents} and business semantics~\cite{lei2025spider2,floratou2024nl2sql,li2023bird,sequeda2024kg,huo2026birdinteract,jiang2025siriusbi} support data agents. DataLab extracts SQL/Python logic and updates notebook dependencies through a DSL~\cite{weng2025datalab}. MetricFlow and Databricks centralize metrics~\cite{dbt2026metricflow,databricks2026metricviews}. MetricFlow validates configurations and executability~\cite{dbt2026metricflowvalidation}; \system\ adds learned, snapshot-specific grain/join evidence (Table~\ref{tab:capabilities}).}
{持久数据库知识~\cite{liu2026agents,luo2026dataagents}与显式业务语义~\cite{lei2025spider2,floratou2024nl2sql,li2023bird,sequeda2024kg,huo2026birdinteract,jiang2025siriusbi}支持数据智能体。DataLab提取SQL／Python逻辑，通过DSL更新笔记本依赖~\cite{weng2025datalab}。MetricFlow与Databricks集中管理声明指标~\cite{dbt2026metricflow,databricks2026metricviews}；MetricFlow验证配置和可执行性~\cite{dbt2026metricflowvalidation}；\system{}增加定义学习及针对查询快照的粒度／连接证据（表~\ref{tab:capabilities}）。}
\bi{\emph{Relational prediction.} RelBench~\cite{robinson2024relbench} supports relational deep learning~\cite{fey2024rdl} and agent feature engineering~\cite{hollmann2023caafe,guo2024dsagent}. FeatPilot, CtxPipe, DeepPrep, and KnowTrans explore or transfer feature preparation~\cite{liang2025featpilot,gao2024ctxpipe,fan2026deepprep,ge2025knowtrans}. \system\ adds database-local reuse with per-instance temporal bounds and consumer-side quality/leakage audits.}
{\emph{关系预测。}RelBench~\cite{robinson2024relbench}支撑关系深度学习~\cite{fey2024rdl}和智能体特征工程~\cite{hollmann2023caafe,guo2024dsagent}。FeatPilot、CtxPipe、DeepPrep和KnowTrans探索或迁移特征准备~\cite{liang2025featpilot,gao2024ctxpipe,fan2026deepprep,ge2025knowtrans}。\system{}在库内复用中加入逐实例时间边界及消费者侧质量／泄漏审计。}
\paragraph{\bt{Database work sharing and validation}{数据库工作共享与验证}}
\bi{Work sharing spans multi-query execution~\cite{sellis1988mqo,harizopoulos2005qpipe,giannikis2012shareddb}, result recycling~\cite{ivanova2010recycling}, semantic caching~\cite{dar1996semantic}, update invalidation~\cite{garrod2008ferdinand}, dependency keys~\cite{gunda2010nectar}, leases~\cite{nishtala2013memcache}, and transactional caches~\cite{ports2010txcache}. Incremental~\cite{gupta1995maintenance,blakeley1986efficiently}, deferred~\cite{colby1996deferred}, and lazy maintenance~\cite{zhou2007lazy}, plus dependency traces~\cite{mokhov2018build,hammer2014adapton}, preserve reusable computation. Summarizability~\cite{lenz1997summarizability,mazon2009summarizability,horner2004additivity}, integrity checking~\cite{nicolas1982integrity,ceri1990constraint}, and validation tools~\cite{schelter2018deequ,breck2019validation,dbt2026tests} underpin validation. \system\ derives conditions from learned definitions and shares versioned evidence.}
{工作共享包括多查询执行~\cite{sellis1988mqo,harizopoulos2005qpipe,giannikis2012shareddb}、结果回收~\cite{ivanova2010recycling}、语义缓存~\cite{dar1996semantic}、更新失效~\cite{garrod2008ferdinand}、依赖键~\cite{gunda2010nectar}、租约~\cite{nishtala2013memcache}与事务缓存~\cite{ports2010txcache}。增量~\cite{gupta1995maintenance,blakeley1986efficiently}、延迟~\cite{colby1996deferred}、惰性维护~\cite{zhou2007lazy}及依赖轨迹~\cite{mokhov2018build,hammer2014adapton}保留可复用计算。汇总性~\cite{lenz1997summarizability,mazon2009summarizability,horner2004additivity}、完整性检查~\cite{nicolas1982integrity,ceri1990constraint}和验证工具~\cite{schelter2018deequ,breck2019validation,dbt2026tests}提供验证基础。\system{}从学得定义派生条件并共享版本化证据，。}
