\section{\bt{Discussion}{讨论}}
\label{sec:limitations}
\bi{\system\ guarantees that a declared use runs on data that satisfies its revision's conditions; business evidence supplies the meaning, and the conditions cover grain, join cardinality and completeness, and the date role. Value semantics (the unit change), a valid but wrong date key, and undeclared SQL lie outside the guarantee, and an ambiguous repair goes to a human with its candidates. Regression needs as-of queries at learning time, through time travel or a retained copy. One built-in learner feeds one admission channel for many consumers; candidates that disagree in structure are kept apart, and reconciling the meanings that different producers give one name is left to people. Rule rewrites are equivalent wherever the maintained conditions hold, whereas an LLM-proposed rewrite rests on sampled equivalence, and adoption measures cost on one snapshot. The changes in our studies are injected, and the end-to-end studies use one LLM with three runs per method. The fixes that consumers find while a definition is invalidated could enter admission as candidate repairs, so that the layer pays for each fix once.}
{\system\ 保证声明了修订的使用在满足该修订条件的数据上执行；业务证据提供含义，条件覆盖粒度、连接基数与连接完整性以及日期角色。取值语义（单位变化）、合法但错误的日期键以及未声明的 SQL 不在保证范围内；有歧义的修复带着候选交给人确认。回归需要按学习时刻作 as-of 查询（time travel 或保留的副本）。一个内置学习者经由同一个准入通道为众多使用者产生候选；结构不一致的候选分开保存，不同生产者对同一名称给出的含义由人来调和。规则改写在受维护的条件成立处都等价，大模型提出的改写则依赖抽样等价，采纳时的代价在一个快照上测得。实验中的变化是注入的，端到端实验使用一个大模型、每种方法 3 次运行。使用者在定义失效期间找到的修正可以作为候选修复进入准入，使这一层对每个修正只付一次。}
