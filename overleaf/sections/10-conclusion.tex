\section{\bt{Discussion and Conclusion}{讨论与结论}}
\label{sec:limitations}
\bi{\system\ reduces repeated exploration by placing shared memory between data agents and the database. Executable definitions learned in one session serve later agents and tasks, amortizing discovery, SQL construction, validation, and optimization across the workload. On TPC-DS, \PpAgents\ agents use \PpMavraTokSaveNone\% fewer tokens and achieve a \PpMavraSpeedupNone$\times$ end-to-end speedup relative to no memory. On six RelBench retraining tasks, token consumption falls by \RbStackMavraTokSave\% on rel-stack and \RbTrialMavraTokSave\% on rel-trial from the second cutoff onward, with comparable predictive quality. Publication checks and shared maintenance additionally improve and preserve answer correctness: TPC-DS accuracy rises from \PpNoneAcc\% to \PpMavraAcc\% initially and reaches \PpMavraChangeAcc\% after a restatement, compared with \PpNoneChangeAcc\% without memory.}
{\system\ 在数据智能体与数据库之间设置共享记忆层，减少重复探索。一个会话学得的可执行定义服务后续智能体与任务，将发现、SQL 构造、验证及优化开销分摊到整个负载。在 TPC-DS 上，\PpAgents\ 个智能体相对无记忆减少 \PpMavraTokSaveNone\% 的 token，获得 \PpMavraSpeedupNone\ 倍端到端加速。在六项 RelBench 重训任务上，从第二个截点起，在 rel-stack 与 rel-trial 上分别减少 \RbStackMavraTokSave\% 与 \RbTrialMavraTokSave\% 的 token，同时保持相当的预测质量。发布检查与共享维护还提高并维护答案正确性：TPC-DS 初始正确率从 \PpNoneAcc\% 提高到 \PpMavraAcc\%，数据更正后达到 \PpMavraChangeAcc\%，无记忆为 \PpNoneChangeAcc\%。}

\bi{\emph{Future work.} Building on star and snowflake metric definitions and entity-level aggregate features, future work will extend compilation to repeated-role joins and many-to-many bridges, and develop incremental feature maintenance. To address the unit/backup boundary in Section~\ref{sec:changes-eval}, we will add explicit units and authoritative-source constraints, alongside checks on filter columns and value distributions, to support semantic reconciliation and drift detection.}
{\emph{未来工作。}在星型与雪花型指标定义及实体级聚合特征的基础上，未来工作将把编译扩展到同表多角色连接和多对多桥接表，并探索特征的增量维护。针对第~\ref{sec:changes-eval} 节的单位／备份副本边界，我们将加入显式量纲与权威来源约束，并检查过滤列和取值分布，以支持业务语义协调与漂移检测。}
