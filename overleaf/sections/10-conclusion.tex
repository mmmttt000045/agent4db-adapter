\section{\bt{Discussion and Conclusion}{讨论与结论}}
\label{sec:limitations}
\bi{\emph{Scope and future work.} The lifecycle checks modeled data conditions under explicit business assumptions; it does not establish arbitrary business semantics from data alone. The filter-column and value-change failures in Section~\ref{sec:changes-eval} motivate richer domain specifications. Further work will extend star and snowflake definitions to repeated-role joins and many-to-many bridges, and develop incremental feature maintenance.}
{\emph{范围与未来工作。}这套生命周期在明确业务假设下检查已建模的数据条件，不能仅凭数据确定任意业务语义。第~\ref{sec:changes-eval} 节中过滤列与取值变化导致的失效表明需要更丰富的领域规约。后续工作将把星型与雪花型定义扩展到同表多角色连接及多对多桥接表，并探索特征的增量维护。}

\bi{\system\ connects publication from verified sessions to shared validation, execution-snapshot checks, and repair or revocation of executable metric definitions. RelBench evaluates the reuse of temporally bounded features and task programs; metric workloads evaluate shared upkeep and update robustness. Together, these studies characterize the cost and validity of maintaining definitions for multiple consumers.}
{\system\ 将核实会话的发布、共享验证、执行快照检查，以及可执行指标定义的修复或撤销连接起来。RelBench 评估受时间边界约束的特征与任务方案的复用；指标负载评估共享维护和更新鲁棒性。这些研究共同刻画了为多个消费者维护定义的代价与有效性。}
