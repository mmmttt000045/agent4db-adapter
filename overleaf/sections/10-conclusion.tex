\section{\bt{Discussion and Conclusion}{讨论与结论}}
\label{sec:limitations}
\bi{\system\ lets database knowledge accumulate across agent sessions. A successful exploration contributes executable definitions that later agents can use for new questions and prediction instances, while the shared layer maintains their conditions and distributes improvements. The evaluation shows both the savings and the work needed to sustain them: the \PpAgents-agent TPC-DS workload uses \PpMavraTokSaveNone\% fewer tokens and runs \PpMavraSpeedupNone$\times$ faster than no memory, and RelBench reuses features across six tasks with comparable predictive quality. Data-change experiments show where structural validation preserves useful reuse and where value semantics require additional information.}
{\system\ 让数据库知识在智能体会话之间积累。一次成功探索贡献可执行定义，后续智能体将其用于新问题和预测样本，共享层则维护定义的条件并传播改进。实验既展示节省，也揭示维持这些节省所需的工作：\PpAgents\ 个智能体的 TPC-DS 负载相对无记忆少用 \PpMavraTokSaveNone\% 的 token，快 \PpMavraSpeedupNone\ 倍；RelBench 在六项任务间复用特征，并保持相当的预测质量。数据变化实验进一步说明结构验证何时能维持有效复用，以及哪些值域语义需要额外信息。}

\bi{\emph{Future work.} Building on star and snowflake metric definitions and entity-level aggregate features, future work will extend compilation to repeated-role joins and many-to-many bridges, and develop incremental feature maintenance. To address the unit/backup boundary in Section~\ref{sec:changes-eval}, we will add explicit units and authoritative-source constraints, alongside checks on filter columns and value distributions, to support semantic reconciliation and drift detection.}
{\emph{未来工作。}在星型与雪花型指标定义及实体级聚合特征的基础上，未来工作将把编译扩展到同表多角色连接和多对多桥接表，并探索特征的增量维护。针对第~\ref{sec:changes-eval} 节的单位／备份副本边界，我们将加入显式量纲与权威来源约束，并检查过滤列和取值分布，以支持业务语义协调与漂移检测。}
