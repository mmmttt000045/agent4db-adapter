\section{\bt{Discussion and Conclusion}{讨论与结论}}
\label{sec:limitations}
\bi{\system\ guarantees that a declared use runs on data that satisfies its revision's conditions, which cover grain, join cardinality and completeness, and the date role; business evidence supplies the meaning. They apply to star and snowflake schemas with a date dimension or a date column; a table joined twice in different roles and many-to-many bridges are outside the implementation. Value semantics (the unit change), a valid but wrong date key, and undeclared SQL lie outside the guarantee, and an ambiguous repair goes to a human with its candidates. Regression needs as-of queries at learning time, through time travel or a retained copy. One built-in learner feeds one admission channel for many consumers; reconciling the meanings that different producers give one name is left to people. The changes in our studies are injected, on four schemas for the library replay; the end-to-end scenarios use one LLM with five runs per method, and the lifecycle study one run. The fixes that consumers find while a definition is invalidated could enter admission as candidate repairs, so that the layer pays for each fix once.}
{\system\ 保证声明了修订的使用在满足该修订条件的数据上执行，条件覆盖粒度、连接基数与连接完整性以及日期角色；业务证据提供含义。条件适用于以日期维度或日期列给出日期的星型与雪花型模式；同一张表以不同角色连接两次、多对多的桥接表不在实现范围内。取值语义（单位变化）、合法但错误的日期键以及未声明的 SQL 不在保证范围内；有歧义的修复带着候选交给人确认。回归需要按学习时刻作 as-of 查询（time travel 或保留的副本）。一个内置学习者经由同一个准入通道为众多使用者产生候选；不同生产者对同一名称给出的含义由人来调和。实验中的变化是注入的，定义库回放覆盖四种模式；端到端情形实验使用一个大模型、每种方法 5 次运行，生命周期实验每种方法 1 次。使用者在定义失效期间找到的修正可以作为候选修复进入准入，使这一层对每个修正只付一次。}

\bi{Data agents repeat knowledge rather than queries. \system\ keeps that knowledge as revisions whose executable conditions are checked against the database they describe: it publishes only what passes admission, lets a use rely on a revision only on a snapshot on which its conditions hold, and maintains and improves revisions once for all consumers. The memory makes agents as accurate as retrieved examples on unchanged data, more accurate when the data changes, and faster throughout.}
{数据智能体重复的是知识而非查询。\system\ 把这些知识保存为修订，其可执行条件对照所描述的数据库检查：只发布通过准入的内容；只允许一次使用在修订条件成立的快照上依赖该修订；为所有使用者一次性地维护和改进修订。这一记忆在数据不变时让智能体与检索到的示例一样准确，在数据变化时更准确，并且始终更快。}
