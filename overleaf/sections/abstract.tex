\begin{abstract}
\bi{Data agents acquire business knowledge through database exploration, but a successful trajectory does not establish whether its assumptions survive later data changes. Declarative semantic layers make knowledge executable and manageable, yet require definitions to be supplied in advance. We present \system, a shared memory layer that turns agent discoveries into a learned semantic layer of executable metric and feature definitions. Checked extraction validates proposed definitions against session evidence, including the business basis and verified answers for metrics. Snapshot-bound compilation and execution instantiate it for a new question and validate its structural conditions on the consuming query's snapshot. Dependency-versioned checks, repairs, and optimizations are shared across agents. We establish sufficient conditions for aggregation correctness and for reusing check results across snapshots. On TPC-DS, \system\ reaches \PpMavraChangeAcc\% accuracy after a data restatement, compared with \PpNoneChangeAcc\% without memory. Across \PpAgents\ agents, it reduces token consumption by \PpMavraTokSaveNone\% and achieves a \PpMavraSpeedupNone$\times$ end-to-end speedup, while using \PpMavraTokSavePrivate\% fewer tokens than private memory. On six RelBench retraining tasks, token use from the second cutoff onward falls by \RbStackMavraTokSave\% on rel-stack and \RbTrialMavraTokSave\% on rel-trial, with comparable predictive quality. Data-change studies also identify value-semantic changes beyond the structural conditions' coverage.}
{数据智能体通过数据库探索获得业务知识，但一次成功轨迹并不能说明其中的前提在后续数据变化后仍然成立。声明式语义层让知识可执行、可管理，却要求预先提供定义。本文提出 \system，一个将智能体发现转化为可执行指标与特征定义、逐步建立语义层的共享记忆层。受检提取依据会话证据验证候选定义，其中指标定义须与业务依据和已核实答案一致；快照绑定的编译与执行为新问题实例化定义，并在消费查询的快照上验证结构条件。检查结果、修复与优化按依赖版本在智能体之间共享。本文给出聚合正确性与跨快照复用检查结果的充分条件。在 TPC-DS 数据更正后，\system\ 的正确率达到 \PpMavraChangeAcc\%，无记忆为 \PpNoneChangeAcc\%。在 \PpAgents\ 个智能体上，它减少 \PpMavraTokSaveNone\% 的 token，获得 \PpMavraSpeedupNone\ 倍端到端加速，相对私有记忆少用 \PpMavraTokSavePrivate\% 的 token。在六项 RelBench 重训任务上，从第二个截点起，rel-stack 与 rel-trial 的 token 分别减少 \RbStackMavraTokSave\% 和 \RbTrialMavraTokSave\%，预测质量相当。数据变化实验也识别出结构条件尚未覆盖的值域语义变化。}
\end{abstract}
