\begin{abstract}
\bi{Data agents repeatedly inspect schemas, probe values, and validate joins when querying the same database. Prior work reuses trajectories, workflows, composite tools, and business logic extracted from historical scripts. Maintaining the data conditions under which executable definitions remain valid is a distinct challenge. We present \system, a database-side memory layer that publishes metric and feature definitions from verified agent sessions. For metrics, the system derives executable conditions from grain and joins, shares check results by dependency version, and checks those conditions on the query's execution snapshot. Failed conditions trigger repair or revocation; the guarantees cover modeled conditions under explicit business assumptions. Feature definitions are compiled with per-instance temporal bounds to prevent future-data leakage. Under periodic retraining on RelBench's \texttt{rel-stack} and \texttt{rel-trial} databases, \system\ reduces token consumption from the second cutoff onward by \RbStackMavraTokSave\% and \RbTrialMavraTokSave\%, respectively, relative to memoryless agents, and by \RbStackMavraVsBest\% on both databases relative to the most token-efficient evaluated agent-memory baseline, while maintaining comparable predictive quality across all six tasks. On TPC-DS, \PpAgents\ agents using \system\ consume \PpMavraTokSaveNone\% fewer tokens than memoryless agents; after a data restatement, they achieve \PpMavraChangeAcc\% accuracy compared with \PpNoneChangeAcc\% for memoryless agents.}
{数据智能体查询同一数据库时，会反复检查模式、探查取值并验证连接。已有工作复用轨迹、工作流、复合工具以及从历史脚本提取的业务逻辑。维护可执行定义赖以有效的数据条件，是另一项需要解决的挑战。本文提出 \system，一个从经过核实的智能体会话发布指标与特征定义的数据库侧记忆层。对于指标，系统从粒度与连接推导可执行条件，按依赖版本共享检查结果，并在查询执行快照上检查这些条件。条件失效触发修复或撤销；其保证覆盖明确业务假设下已建模的条件。特征定义被编译为带逐样本时间边界的 SQL，以防止未来数据泄漏。在 RelBench 的 \texttt{rel-stack} 与 \texttt{rel-trial} 数据库的定期重训任务中，从第二个截点起，\system\ 相对无记忆智能体分别减少 \RbStackMavraTokSave\% 与 \RbTrialMavraTokSave\% 的 token，相对各库中已评估的 token 用量最低的智能体记忆基线均减少 \RbStackMavraVsBest\%，同时在全部六项任务上保持相当的预测质量。在 TPC-DS 上，\PpAgents\ 个使用 \system\ 的智能体比无记忆智能体少消耗 \PpMavraTokSaveNone\% 的 token；在数据更正后，其正确率为 \PpMavraChangeAcc\%，而无记忆智能体为 \PpNoneChangeAcc\%。}
\end{abstract}
