\bi{\emph{Setup (Q8).} Unlike the scenarios, which roll back every change, here one library per method lives through \LcSteps\ cumulative steps on one million rows (Table~\ref{tab:lifecycle}); each step applies its update and sends a batch of \LcWave\ new user agents, \LcConc\ at a time, asking the first three scored question types of every metric. \system\ runs in its full configuration, and its learning agent relearns any metric left without a valid definition. \emph{Result.} \system\ answers \LcAccCond\% of the \LcTasks\ questions, example retrieval \LcAccTraj\%, and agents without memory \LcAccNone\%. \system\ repaired \LcRepaired\ definitions; when the re-keying broke the assumption of the \LcOptimized\ optimized definitions, maintenance on the seven that consumers requested restored \LcByPred\ previous revisions and undid the rule for \LcByUndo\ revisions repaired since, and \LcRekeyCorrectCond\ of \LcWave\ answers stayed correct; it invalidated \LcRevoked\ definitions under the duplicate load and relearned their metrics after the cleanup, refusing \LcRelearnRefused\ faulty extracted candidates on the way. After the duplicate load, the stale examples of retrieval answer \LcAfterCorrectMaxTraj\ of \LcWave\ in both steps, against \LcAfterCorrectMinCond\ and \LcAfterCorrectMaxCond\ for \system.}
{\emph{设置（Q8）。}与每次变化后回滚的场景不同，这里每种方法的一个定义库在 100 万行数据上依次经历 \LcSteps\ 个累积的步骤（表~\ref{tab:lifecycle}）；每一步施加更新后来一波 \LcWave\ 个新使用者，\LcConc\ 个同时进行，问每个指标的前三种计分题型。\system\ 使用完整配置，其学习智能体重新学习没有有效定义的指标。\emph{结果。}\system\ 答对 \LcTasks\ 道题中的 \LcAccCond\%，示例检索 \LcAccTraj\%，无记忆的智能体 \LcAccNone\%。\system\ 修复了 \LcRepaired\ 个定义；日期键重编号破坏 \LcOptimized\ 个优化定义的前提时，对使用者请求到的 7 个定义的维护恢复了 \LcByPred\ 个前一版本，对此后被修复过的 \LcByUndo\ 个版本撤销改写规则，\LcWave\ 个答案中 \LcRekeyCorrectCond\ 个保持正确；重复加载下它使 \LcRevoked\ 个定义失效，并在清理之后重新学习这些指标，途中拒绝了 \LcRelearnRefused\ 个提取有误的候选。重复加载之后的两步中，检索过期的示例都只答对 \LcWave\ 道中的 \LcAfterCorrectMaxTraj\ 道，\system\ 为 \LcAfterCorrectMinCond\ 道和 \LcAfterCorrectMaxCond\ 道。}
