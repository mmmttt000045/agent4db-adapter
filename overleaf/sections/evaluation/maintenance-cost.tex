\subsection{\bt{Maintenance cost}{维护代价}}
\label{sec:cost}
\bi{\emph{Controlled benchmark (Q4).} To isolate database maintenance, the benchmark admits structured definitions directly through the admission checks: up to $k\in\{1,2,4,6\}$ definitions from each of four families (sales, returns, return ratios, catalog), 4 to 19 in all, with $k=1$ retaining sharing between the sales and return-ratio families. After each of four updates, three benign appends and the status-change rows of Section~\ref{sec:background} (repaired under either regression reference), eight scripted agents each use every definition in shuffled order, with staggered or simultaneous arrivals, and database time sums the operations during these uses. Besides the full recheck per definition and \system, we run a variant that rechecks changed tables only, skipping conditions on unchanged tables but reusing no check results, and the query cache, which caches the check queries of the full recheck by normalized SQL text and table versions, with no knowledge of condition types.}
{\emph{受控实验（Q4）。}为单独测量数据库维护，基准把结构化定义直接通过准入检查准入：四个族（销售、退货、退货比率、目录）各取最多 $k\in\{1,2,4,6\}$ 个定义，共 4 到 19 个，$k=1$ 仍保留销售与退货比率族之间的共享。四次更新（三次正常追加与第~\ref{sec:background} 节的状态变更行，两种回归参照下都能修复）之后，8 个脚本智能体各按打乱的顺序使用所有定义，采用错峰或同时到达，数据库时间累计这些使用期间的操作。除整定义重查与 \system\ 外，还运行只重查变化的表的变体（跳过未变化表上的条件，但不复用检查结果）与查询缓存（按规范化 SQL 文本与表版本缓存整定义重查的检查查询，不了解条件类型）。}

\bi{Figure~\ref{fig:cost} shows that check-result reuse, not scope, provides the savings, and that it does not require typed conditions. Under staggered arrivals, the full recheck's time grows with the number of definitions (\CbStagDefOne\ to \CbStagDefSix\,s), whereas \system\ and the query cache stay at \CbCondStagMin--\CbCondStagMax\ and \CbCacheStagMin--\CbCacheStagMax\,s; with 19 definitions they need \CbStagCondSix\% and \CbStagCacheSix\% of the full recheck's time, while rechecking changed tables only needs 83--94\%. The one visible gap appears with the least sharing (\CbStagCondOne\% vs.\ \CbStagCacheOne\%): the return-ratio grain condition is the condition that a join cardinality check has just checked with its key columns in another order, which \system's canonical form recognizes and a cache keyed by SQL text does not. Under simultaneous arrivals, request coalescing already removes much duplicate work for every method, and the cache and \system\ need 50--84\% of the full recheck's time. With three definitions that share no condition, all methods cost \CbZeroMin--\CbZeroMax\% of the full recheck's time: the savings come from sharing alone, and tracking conditions adds no visible overhead. All cells record zero stale uses, false invalidations, and unavailable uses.}
{图~\ref{fig:cost} 表明，节省来自检查结果复用而不是缩小检查范围，而且检查结果复用不需要类型化条件。错峰到达时，整定义重查的耗时随定义数增长（\CbStagDefOne\ 到 \CbStagDefSix\ 秒），\system\ 与查询缓存则分别保持在 \CbCondStagMin--\CbCondStagMax\ 与 \CbCacheStagMin--\CbCacheStagMax\ 秒；19 个定义时二者分别只需整定义重查的 \CbStagCondSix\% 与 \CbStagCacheSix\%，只重查变化的表需要 83--94\%。唯一明显的差距出现在共享最少时（\CbStagCondOne\% 对 \CbStagCacheOne\%）：退货比率的粒度条件与连接基数检查刚刚以另一种键列顺序检查过的条件是同一个，\system\ 的规范形式能识别这一点，按 SQL 文本索引的缓存则不能。同时到达时，请求合并已为各方法去掉了大量重复工作，查询缓存与 \system\ 需要整定义重查的 50--84\%。三个定义互不共享条件时，各方法的耗时都在整定义重查的 \CbZeroMin--\CbZeroMax\%：节省只来自共享，跟踪条件也没有可见的额外开销。所有配置都是零过期使用、零误失效、零不可用。}

\bi{\emph{Scale and concurrency.} The ratios persist with data size and agent count: with 4 million rows, \system\ and the cache need \CbFourStagCond\% and \CbFourStagCache\% of the full recheck's time under staggered arrivals, with 32 agents \CbAgentsStagCond\% and \CbAgentsStagCache\%, and from 1 to 16 million rows \system's share of the full recheck's time stays at \EoneRatioSixteen--\EoneRatioFour\% (\EoneCondSixteen\ versus \EoneDefSixteen\,s at 16 million rows). Simultaneous requests from 32 agents leave little to save beyond request coalescing (\CbAgentsBurstCond\% and \CbAgentsBurstCache\%).}
{\emph{规模与并发。}这些比例在数据量与智能体数增加后保持不变：400 万行时，错峰到达下 \system\ 与查询缓存分别需要整定义重查的 \CbFourStagCond\% 与 \CbFourStagCache\%；32 个智能体时为 \CbAgentsStagCond\% 与 \CbAgentsStagCache\%；从 100 万到 1600 万行，\system\ 占整定义重查耗时的比例稳定在 \EoneRatioSixteen--\EoneRatioFour\%（1600 万行时 \EoneCondSixteen\ 秒对 \EoneDefSixteen\ 秒）。32 个智能体同时到达时，除请求合并外已没有多少可省（\CbAgentsBurstCond\% 与 \CbAgentsBurstCache\%）。}
