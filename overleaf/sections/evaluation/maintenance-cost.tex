\subsection{\bt{Maintenance cost}{维护代价}}
\label{sec:cost}
\bi{\emph{Controlled benchmark (Q4).} To isolate database maintenance, the benchmark admits structured definitions directly through the validation gates: up to $k\in\{1,2,4,6\}$ definitions from each of four families (sales, returns, return ratios, catalog), 4 to 19 in all, with $k=1$ retaining sharing between the sales and return-ratio families. After each of four updates, three benign appends and the status-history break of Section~\ref{sec:background}, eight scripted agents each use every definition in shuffled order, with staggered or simultaneous arrivals, and database time sums the operations during these uses. Besides definition-level revalidation and \system, we run scope-only maintenance, which skips conditions on unchanged tables but reuses no verdicts, and the generic cache, which caches the check queries of definition-level revalidation by normalized SQL text and table versions, with no knowledge of condition types.}
{\emph{受控实验（Q4）。}为单独测量数据库维护，基准把结构化定义直接通过验证门槛准入：四个族（销售、退货、退货比率、目录）各取最多 $k\in\{1,2,4,6\}$ 个定义，共 4 到 19 个，$k=1$ 仍保留销售与退货比率族之间的共享。四次更新（三次正常追加与第~\ref{sec:background} 节的状态流水破坏）之后，8 个脚本智能体各按打乱的顺序使用所有定义，采用错峰或同时到达，数据库时间累计这些使用期间的操作。除定义级重验证与 \system\ 外，还运行仅范围维护（跳过未变化表上的条件，但不复用结论）与通用缓存（按规范化 SQL 文本与表版本缓存定义级重验证的检查查询，不了解条件类型）。}

\bi{Figure~\ref{fig:cost} shows that verdict reuse, not scope, provides the savings, and that it does not require typed conditions. Under staggered arrivals, definition-level time grows with the number of definitions (\CbStagDefOne\ to \CbStagDefSix\,s), whereas \system\ and the generic cache stay at \CbCondStagMin--\CbCondStagMax\ and \CbCacheStagMin--\CbCacheStagMax\,s; with 19 definitions they need \CbStagCondSix\% and \CbStagCacheSix\% of the definition-level time, while scope-only maintenance needs 83--94\%. The one visible gap appears with the least sharing (\CbStagCondOne\% vs.\ \CbStagCacheOne\%): the return-ratio grain condition is the condition that a join guard has just checked with its key columns in another order, which \system's semantic identity recognizes and a cache keyed by SQL text does not. Under simultaneous arrivals, in-flight merging already removes much duplicate work for every method, and the cache and \system\ need 50--84\% of definition-level time. With three definitions that share no condition, all methods cost \CbZeroMin--\CbZeroMax\% of definition-level time: the savings come from sharing alone, and tracking conditions adds no visible overhead. All cells record zero stale uses, false revocations, and unavailable uses.}
{图~\ref{fig:cost} 表明，节省来自结论复用而不是缩小检查范围，而且结论复用不需要类型化条件。错峰到达时，定义级的耗时随定义数增长（\CbStagDefOne\ 到 \CbStagDefSix\ 秒），\system\ 与通用缓存则分别保持在 \CbCondStagMin--\CbCondStagMax\ 与 \CbCacheStagMin--\CbCacheStagMax\ 秒；19 个定义时二者分别只需定义级的 \CbStagCondSix\% 与 \CbStagCacheSix\%，仅范围维护需要 83--94\%。唯一明显的差距出现在共享最少时（\CbStagCondOne\% 对 \CbStagCacheOne\%）：退货比率的粒度条件与连接守卫刚刚以另一种键列顺序检查过的条件是同一个，\system\ 的语义身份能识别这一点，按 SQL 文本索引的缓存则不能。同时到达时，在途合并已为各方法去掉了大量重复工作，通用缓存与 \system\ 需要定义级的 50--84\%。三个定义互不共享条件时，各方法的耗时都在定义级的 \CbZeroMin--\CbZeroMax\%：节省只来自共享，跟踪条件也没有可见的额外开销。所有配置都是零过期使用、零误撤销、零不可用。}

\bi{\emph{Scale and concurrency.} The ratios persist with data size and agent count: with 4 million rows, \system\ and the cache need \CbFourStagCond\% and \CbFourStagCache\% of definition-level time under staggered arrivals, with 32 agents \CbAgentsStagCond\% and \CbAgentsStagCache\%, and from 1 to 16 million rows the condition-level share of definition-level time stays at \EoneRatioSixteen--\EoneRatioFour\% (\EoneCondSixteen\ versus \EoneDefSixteen\,s at 16 million rows). Simultaneous requests from 32 agents leave little to save beyond in-flight merging (\CbAgentsBurstCond\% and \CbAgentsBurstCache\%).}
{\emph{规模与并发。}这些比例在数据量与智能体数增加后保持不变：400 万行时，错峰到达下 \system\ 与通用缓存分别需要定义级的 \CbFourStagCond\% 与 \CbFourStagCache\%；32 个智能体时为 \CbAgentsStagCond\% 与 \CbAgentsStagCache\%；从 100 万到 1600 万行，条件级占定义级耗时的比例稳定在 \EoneRatioSixteen--\EoneRatioFour\%（1600 万行时 \EoneCondSixteen\ 秒对 \EoneDefSixteen\ 秒）。32 个智能体同时到达时，除在途合并外已没有多少可省（\CbAgentsBurstCond\% 与 \CbAgentsBurstCache\%）。}
