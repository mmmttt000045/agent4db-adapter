\subsection{\bt{Maintenance and optimization once for all agents}{为所有智能体一次性维护与优化}}
\label{sec:cost}
\bi{\emph{Who pays for correctness (Q4).} \system\ pays for maintenance once per change, for all agents: \AmMaintBenignS\,s of database time per non-breaking change and \AmMaintRepairS\,s per repaired one in the end-to-end runs. Leaving verification to each agent costs every agent: the self-verifying retrieval agent spends \AmVerifyExtraS\,s and \AmVerifyExtraTokK\,k tokens more per question even on unchanged data, and remains less accurate under breaking changes. Shared check results keep the maintenance itself small, because definitions share conditions: an update of \code{store\_sales} touches \TpSalesDefs\ of the \TpDefs\ definitions derived from the TPC-DS templates but only \TpSalesConds\ distinct conditions. }
{\emph{正确性由谁付出代价（Q4）。}\system\ 每次变化只为所有智能体维护一次：端到端实验中，每次正常更新 \AmMaintBenignS\ 秒数据库时间，每次需要修复的变化 \AmMaintRepairS\ 秒。把核验留给每个智能体，代价就落在每个智能体身上：自行核验的检索智能体即使在数据不变时，每题也多花 \AmVerifyExtraS\ 秒和 \AmVerifyExtraTokK\,k token，在破坏性变化下仍然不如 \system\ 准确。共享的检查结果使维护本身很小，因为定义之间共享条件：更新 \code{store\_sales} 会触及从 TPC-DS 模板导出的 \TpDefs\ 个定义中的 \TpSalesDefs\ 个，但不同条件只有 \TpSalesConds\ 个。}

\bi{\emph{Controlled benchmark.} To measure what shared check results save as definitions accumulate, a benchmark publishes $k$ structured definitions, $k\in\{1,2,4,6\}$, from each of four families (sales, returns, return ratios, catalog), 4 to 19 in all, with $k=1$ retaining sharing between the sales and return-ratio families. After each of four updates, three non-breaking appends and the status-change rows, eight scripted agents use every definition in shuffled order, with staggered or simultaneous arrivals, and database time sums the operations during these uses. Besides the full recheck per definition and \system, we run a variant that rechecks changed tables only, reusing no check results, and a query cache over the full recheck's check queries keyed by normalized SQL text and table versions. Figure~\ref{fig:cost} shows that check-result reuse, not scope, provides the savings. Under staggered arrivals the full recheck's time grows with the number of definitions (\CbStagDefOne\ to \CbStagDefSix\,s), whereas \system\ stays at \CbCondStagMin--\CbCondStagMax\,s; with 19 definitions it needs \CbStagCondSix\% of the full recheck's time and the query cache \CbStagCacheSix\%, while rechecking changed tables only needs 83--94\%. With the least sharing, \system's normalized form recognizes a condition just checked with its key columns in another order (\CbStagCondOne\% against \CbStagCacheOne\% for the cache). Without shared conditions, all methods cost \CbZeroMin--\CbZeroMax\% of the full recheck's time: the savings come from sharing alone. The ratios persist with data size and agent count: at 4 million rows \system\ needs \CbFourStagCond\% of the full recheck's time, with 32 agents \CbAgentsStagCond\%, and from 1 to 16 million rows its share stays at \EoneRatioSixteen--\EoneRatioFour\% (\EoneCondSixteen\ against \EoneDefSixteen\,s at 16 million rows). All cells record zero stale uses, false invalidations, and unavailable uses.}
{\emph{受控实验。}为了测量定义增多时共享检查结果省下多少，基准从四个族（销售、退货、退货比率、目录）各发布 $k\in\{1,2,4,6\}$ 个结构化定义，共 4 到 19 个，$k=1$ 仍保留销售与退货比率族之间的共享。四次更新（三次正常追加，以及退货改为状态流水）之后，8 个脚本智能体各按打乱的顺序使用所有定义，错峰或同时到达，数据库时间累计这些使用期间的操作。除整定义重查与 \system\ 外，还运行只重查变化的表的变体（不复用检查结果），以及按规范化 SQL 文本与表版本缓存整定义重查检查查询的查询缓存。图~\ref{fig:cost} 表明，节省来自检查结果复用而不是缩小检查范围。错峰到达时，整定义重查的耗时随定义数增长（\CbStagDefOne\ 到 \CbStagDefSix\ 秒），\system\ 则保持在 \CbCondStagMin--\CbCondStagMax\ 秒；19 个定义时它只需整定义重查的 \CbStagCondSix\%，查询缓存为 \CbStagCacheSix\%，只重查变化的表需要 83--94\%。共享最少时，\system\ 的规范化形式能识别以另一种键列顺序刚检查过的同一条件（\CbStagCondOne\%，查询缓存为 \CbStagCacheOne\%）。没有共享条件时，各方法都需要整定义重查的 \CbZeroMin--\CbZeroMax\%：节省只来自共享。这些比例在数据量与智能体数增加后保持不变：400 万行时 \system\ 需要整定义重查的 \CbFourStagCond\%，32 个智能体时为 \CbAgentsStagCond\%，从 100 万到 1600 万行稳定在 \EoneRatioSixteen--\EoneRatioFour\%（1600 万行时 \EoneCondSixteen\ 秒对 \EoneDefSixteen\ 秒）。所有配置都是零过期使用、零误失效、零不可用。}

\label{sec:opt-eval}
\bi{\emph{One optimization speeds up every agent.} We add one optimization pass after learning in one end-to-end run at one million sales rows. \OptPublished\ of the \OptDefs\ published definitions received a key-range revision whose generated SQL ran \OptSaveMin--\OptSaveMax\% faster (mean \OptSaveMean\%, every 95\% interval below zero), with equal results on \OptPeriods\ periods each, and \OptHoldAdopted\ of the \OptHoldTasks\ held-out queries ran the faster form because agents copy the SQL they retrieve. To break the added assumption, a system-level test re-keys one month of the date dimension so that only date-key contiguity fails: maintenance invalidated all \PbDefs\ key-range revisions and restored their previous revisions within \PbFallbackMsMax\,ms each, and all \PbAnswers\ answers served afterwards are correct, whereas the key-range SQL would have been wrong by up to \PbStaleErrMax\%.}
{\emph{一次优化让每个智能体都更快。}我们在一次端到端运行（100 万行销售）的学习之后加入一轮优化。已发布的 \OptDefs\ 个定义中有 \OptPublished\ 个得到键区间版本，其生成的 SQL 快 \OptSaveMin--\OptSaveMax\%（平均 \OptSaveMean\%，每个 95\% 区间都低于零），每个都在 \OptPeriods\ 个期间上结果相同；由于智能体照抄检索到的 SQL，\OptHoldTasks\ 条留出查询中有 \OptHoldAdopted\ 条用了更快的写法。为了破坏新增的前提，一个系统层测试给日期维度的一个月重新编键，使只有日期键连续性失败：维护使全部 \PbDefs\ 个键区间版本失效，并在每个 \PbFallbackMsMax\ 毫秒内恢复其前一版本，之后给出的 \PbAnswers\ 个答案全部正确，而键区间 SQL 的误差会高达 \PbStaleErrMax\%。}

\bi{\emph{A long-lived library.}\label{sec:lifecycle-eval} Unlike the scenarios, which roll back every change, here one library per method lives through \LcSteps\ cumulative steps on one million rows; each step applies its update and sends a batch of \LcWave\ new user agents, \LcConc\ at a time, asking the first three scored question types of every metric. \system\ answers \LcAccCond\% of the \LcTasks\ questions, example retrieval \LcAccTraj\%, and agents without memory \LcAccNone\%. \system\ repaired \LcRepaired\ definitions; when a re-keying of the date dimension broke the assumption of the \LcOptimized\ optimized definitions, maintenance restored \LcByPred\ previous revisions and undid the rule for \LcByUndo\ revisions repaired since, and \LcRekeyCorrectCond\ of \LcWave\ answers stayed correct; it invalidated \LcRevoked\ definitions under a duplicate load and relearned their metrics after the cleanup, refusing \LcRelearnRefused\ faulty extracted candidates on the way. After the duplicate load, the stale examples of retrieval answer \LcAfterCorrectMaxTraj\ of \LcWave\ in both steps, against \LcAfterCorrectMinCond\ and \LcAfterCorrectMaxCond\ for \system.}
{\emph{一个长期使用的定义库。}与每次变化后回滚的场景不同，这里每种方法的一个定义库在 100 万行数据上依次经历 \LcSteps\ 个累积的步骤；每一步施加更新后来一批 \LcWave\ 个新的用户端智能体，\LcConc\ 个同时进行，问每个指标的前三种计分题型。\system\ 答对 \LcTasks\ 道题中的 \LcAccCond\%，示例检索 \LcAccTraj\%，无记忆的智能体 \LcAccNone\%。\system\ 修复了 \LcRepaired\ 个定义；日期维度重新编键破坏 \LcOptimized\ 个优化定义的前提时，维护恢复了 \LcByPred\ 个前一版本，对此后被修复过的 \LcByUndo\ 个版本撤销改写规则，\LcWave\ 个答案中 \LcRekeyCorrectCond\ 个保持正确；重复加载下它使 \LcRevoked\ 个定义失效，并在清理之后重新学习这些指标，途中拒绝了 \LcRelearnRefused\ 个提取有误的候选。重复加载之后的两步中，检索过期的示例都只答对 \LcWave\ 道中的 \LcAfterCorrectMaxTraj\ 道，\system\ 为 \LcAfterCorrectMinCond\ 道和 \LcAfterCorrectMaxCond\ 道。}
