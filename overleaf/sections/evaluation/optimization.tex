\subsection{\bt{Maintenance and optimization once for all agents}{为所有智能体一次性维护与优化}}
\label{sec:opt-eval}
\label{sec:cost}
\bi{\emph{Who pays for correctness (Q4).} \system\ pays for maintenance once per change, for all agents: \AmMaintBenignS\,s of database time per non-breaking change and \AmMaintRepairS\,s per repaired one in the end-to-end runs. Leaving verification to each agent costs every agent: the self-verifying retrieval agent spends \AmVerifyExtraS\,s and \AmVerifyExtraTokK\,k tokens more per question even on unchanged data, and remains less accurate under breaking changes. Shared check results keep the maintenance itself small, because definitions share conditions: an update of \code{store\_sales} touches \TpSalesDefs\ of the \TpDefs\ definitions derived from the TPC-DS templates but only \TpSalesConds\ distinct conditions. Through a lifecycle of \LcSteps\ accumulating updates with batches of new agents, one library keeps \LcAccCond\% of \LcTasks\ answers correct, against \LcAccTraj\% for example retrieval and \LcAccNone\% without memory.}
{\emph{正确性由谁付出代价（Q4）。}\system\ 每次变化只为所有智能体维护一次：端到端实验中，每次正常更新 \AmMaintBenignS\ 秒数据库时间，每次需要修复的变化 \AmMaintRepairS\ 秒。把核验留给每个智能体，代价就落在每个智能体身上：自行核验的检索智能体即使在数据不变时，每题也多花 \AmVerifyExtraS\ 秒和 \AmVerifyExtraTokK\,k token，在破坏性变化下仍然不如 \system\ 准确。共享的检查结果使维护本身很小，因为定义之间共享条件：更新 \code{store\_sales} 会触及从 TPC-DS 模板导出的 \TpDefs\ 个定义中的 \TpSalesDefs\ 个，但不同条件只有 \TpSalesConds\ 个。在 \LcSteps\ 步不断累积的更新与一批批新来的智能体下，一个定义库保持 \LcAccCond\% 的答案正确（共 \LcTasks\ 题），示例检索为 \LcAccTraj\%，无记忆为 \LcAccNone\%。}

\bi{\emph{One optimization speeds up every agent.} We add one optimization pass after learning in one end-to-end run at one million sales rows. \OptPublished\ of the \OptDefs\ published definitions received a key-range revision whose generated SQL ran \OptSaveMin--\OptSaveMax\% faster (mean \OptSaveMean\%, every 95\% interval below zero), with equal results on \OptPeriods\ periods each, and \OptHoldAdopted\ of the \OptHoldTasks\ held-out queries ran the faster form because agents copy the SQL they retrieve. To break the added assumption, a system-level test re-keys one month of the date dimension so that only date-key contiguity fails: maintenance invalidated all \PbDefs\ key-range revisions and restored their previous revisions within \PbFallbackMsMax\,ms each, and all \PbAnswers\ answers served afterwards are correct, whereas the key-range SQL would have been wrong by up to \PbStaleErrMax\%.}
{\emph{一次优化让每个智能体都更快。}我们在一次端到端运行（100 万行销售）的学习之后加入一轮优化。已发布的 \OptDefs\ 个定义中有 \OptPublished\ 个得到键区间版本，其生成的 SQL 快 \OptSaveMin--\OptSaveMax\%（平均 \OptSaveMean\%，每个 95\% 区间都低于零），每个都在 \OptPeriods\ 个期间上结果相同；由于智能体照抄检索到的 SQL，\OptHoldTasks\ 条留出查询中有 \OptHoldAdopted\ 条用了更快的写法。为了破坏新增的前提，一个系统层测试给日期维度的一个月重新编键，使只有日期键连续性失败：维护使全部 \PbDefs\ 个键区间版本失效，并在每个 \PbFallbackMsMax\ 毫秒内恢复其前一版本，之后给出的 \PbAnswers\ 个答案全部正确，而键区间 SQL 的误差会高达 \PbStaleErrMax\%。}
