\subsection{\bt{Questions and setup}{问题与设置}}
\bi{The evaluation answers five questions. (Q1) Do maintained definitions stay correct under data-only changes, independently of what agents learned? (Q2) Does use-time enforcement hold under concurrent writes, and at what cost? (Q3) When does repair restore a broken definition, and when must it invalidate it? (Q4) What does maintenance cost, and which part of the savings requires typed conditions? (Q5) What do maintained definitions do for LLM agents end to end? Q1--Q4 are answered at the system level, without LLMs, where definitions are fixed and only maintenance varies; Q5 runs complete agents. The machine has 48 Hygon cores and 98\,GiB RAM. PostgreSQL 18.6 runs in disposable isolated databases with WAL and fsync disabled. The synthetic retail fixture uses TPC-DS-style column names; with one million store-sales rows it contains about 396,000 returns and 500,000 catalog-sales rows, and each study states its scale. Table~\ref{tab:ladder} lists the maintenance strategies and the studies that compare them. The end-to-end study adds agents without shared knowledge, trajectory retrieval with and without a self-check instruction, and sharing without validation; the system-level studies add affected-only revalidation and \system\ with G8 on current data. Revalidation methods share conditions, repair, regression test, and request coalescing. G8 compares with the agent's own learning SQL as of learning time, except that definition-level revalidation uses the benchmark's gold SQL end to end, where \system\ with the gold SQL answers the same as \system\ (Section~\ref{sec:scenarios}).}
{评估回答五个问题。（Q1）维护后的定义能否在纯数据变化下保持正确，且不受智能体学到什么的影响？（Q2）存在并发写入时，使用时的强制保证是否成立，代价如何？（Q3）修复何时能恢复被破坏的定义，何时必须使定义失效？（Q4）维护代价如何，节省中哪一部分需要类型化条件？（Q5）维护后的定义在端到端上对大模型智能体有何作用？Q1--Q4 在系统层回答（不调用大模型）：定义固定，只改变维护方式；Q5 运行完整的智能体。实验机有 48 核 Hygon 和 98\,GiB 内存。PostgreSQL 18.6 在可丢弃的隔离库中运行，关闭 WAL 与 fsync。合成零售数据采用 TPC-DS 风格列名；100 万行门店销售对应约 39.6 万行退货和 50 万行目录销售，各实验分别说明其规模。表~\ref{tab:ladder} 列出各维护方式及比较它们的实验。端到端实验另加入没有共享知识的智能体、带与不带自检指令的轨迹检索，以及共享不验证；系统层实验另加入只查受影响条件的重验证和 G8 用当前数据的 \system。各重验证方法共用条件、修复、回归测试与请求合并。G8 以智能体自己的学习 SQL 按学习时刻比较，只有定义级重验证在端到端实验中使用基准的标准答案 SQL，而使用标准答案 SQL 的 \system\ 在那里与 \system\ 答对的题数相同（第~\ref{sec:scenarios} 节）。}
