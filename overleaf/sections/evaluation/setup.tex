\subsection{\bt{Questions and setup}{研究问题与实验设置}}
\bi{The evaluation answers five questions. (Q1) Do maintained definitions stay correct under data-only changes, independently of what agents learned? (Q2) Does use-time enforcement hold under concurrent writes, and at what cost? (Q3) When does repair restore a broken definition, and when must it invalidate it? (Q4) What does maintenance cost, and which part of the savings requires typed conditions? (Q5) What do maintained definitions do for LLM agents end to end? Q1--Q4 are answered at the system level, without LLMs, where definitions are fixed and only maintenance varies; Q5 runs complete agents. The machine has 48 Hygon cores and 98\,GiB RAM. PostgreSQL 18.6 runs in disposable isolated databases with WAL and fsync disabled. The synthetic retail fixture uses TPC-DS-style column names; with one million store-sales rows it contains about 396,000 returns and 500,000 catalog-sales rows, and the other studies scale it to the sizes in Table~\ref{tab:experiments}. Table~\ref{tab:baselines} lists the compared methods.}
{评估回答五个问题。（Q1）维护后的定义能否在纯数据变化下保持正确，且不受智能体学到什么的影响？（Q2）存在并发写入时，使用时的强制保证是否成立，代价如何？（Q3）修复何时能恢复被破坏的定义，何时必须使定义失效？（Q4）维护代价如何，节省中哪一部分需要类型化条件？（Q5）维护后的定义在端到端上对大模型智能体有何作用？Q1--Q4 在系统层回答（不调用大模型）：定义固定，只改变维护方式；Q5 运行完整的智能体。实验机有 48 核 Hygon 和 98\,GiB 内存。PostgreSQL 18.6 在可丢弃的隔离库中运行，关闭 WAL 与 fsync。合成零售数据采用 TPC-DS 风格列名；100 万行门店销售对应约 39.6 万行退货和 50 万行目录销售，其他实验按表~\ref{tab:experiments} 的规模缩放。表~\ref{tab:baselines} 列出被比较的方法。}
