\subsection{\bt{Snapshot binding: validity and overhead}{快照绑定：有效性与开销}}
\label{sec:snapshot-eval}
\bi{\emph{Matched control.} Figure~\ref{fig:snapshot-control}(a) fixes metric SQL, guards, condition cache, transactional versions, and 200,000 synthetic sales rows. Precheck releases the checked snapshot before execution; bound execution retains it. Barriers order benign or grain-breaking commits after validation, or a breaking commit before invocation. Five counterbalanced blocks yield 30 calls without errors. Both modes serve all benign cases and refuse all breaking-before-call cases. Breaking-after-check commits yield five violating precheck results and five valid bound results. These schedules show that retaining the repeatable-read snapshot closes the validation-to-execution race window; synchronization barriers are excluded from timing.}
{\emph{匹配对照。}图~\ref{fig:snapshot-control}(a)固定指标SQL、守卫、条件缓存、事务性版本和200,000行合成销售。预检查执行前释放核验快照，绑定执行保留它。屏障安排核验后提交良性／破坏粒度写入，或调用前提交破坏性写入。五轮交替模式顺序，共30次调用、无错误。两组均接受全部良性情形、拒绝全部调用前破坏情形；检查后破坏时，预检查返回五次违规结果，绑定执行返回五次有效结果。这些调度说明保留可重复读快照关闭了验证到执行之间的竞争窗口；同步屏障不计入性能。}
\bi{\emph{Read and write overhead.} Panels (b,c) use separate reader/writer cohorts. Unbound precheck and bound execution take 5.1/6.9\,ms at steady state and 1,188/1,095.1\,ms for the first read after a benign write. Their version sources differ. At 32 writers, 16 shards retain 96.6\% of no-trigger throughput versus 36.1\% for one row.}
{\emph{读写开销。}子图(b,c)来自独立读／写批次。非绑定预检查与绑定执行的稳态延迟为5.1／6.9\,ms，良性写入后首次读取为1,188／1,095.1\,ms；两者版本来源不同。32个写连接下，16分片保留无触发器吞吐的96.6\%，单行为36.1\%。}
