\subsection{\bt{Snapshot binding: validity and overhead}{快照绑定：有效性与开销}}
\label{sec:snapshot-eval}
\bi{\emph{Matched control.} Figure~\ref{fig:snapshot-control}(a) fixes metric SQL, guards, condition cache, transactional versions, and 200,000 synthetic sales rows. Precheck releases the checked snapshot before execution; bound execution retains it. Barriers order benign or grain-breaking commits after validation, or a breaking commit before invocation. Five counterbalanced blocks yield 30 calls without errors. Both modes serve all benign cases and refuse all breaking-before-call cases. Breaking-after-check commits yield five violating precheck results and five valid bound results. These deterministic schedules identify the snapshot-lifetime mechanism, not natural violation rates; artificial waits are excluded from performance.}
{\emph{匹配对照。}图~\ref{fig:snapshot-control}(a)固定指标SQL、守卫、条件缓存、事务性版本和200,000行合成销售。预检查执行前释放核验快照，绑定执行保留它。屏障安排核验后提交良性／破坏粒度写入，或调用前提交破坏性写入。五轮交替模式顺序，共30次调用、无错误。两组均接受全部良性情形、拒绝全部调用前破坏情形；检查后破坏时，预检查返回五次违规结果，绑定执行返回五次有效结果。这检验快照生命周期，不估计自然违规率；人工等待不计入性能。}
\bi{\emph{Archived overhead.} Panels (b,c) use separate reader/writer cohorts. Legacy precheck and bound execution take 5.1/6.9\,ms at steady state and 1,188/1,095.1\,ms for the first read after a benign write. Their version sources also differ, so this does not isolate binding overhead. At 32 writers, 16 shards retain 96.6\% of no-trigger throughput versus 36.1\% for one row. These costs do not form a joint safety--throughput curve.}
{\emph{归档开销。}子图(b,c)来自独立读／写批次。旧预检查与绑定执行的稳态延迟为5.1／6.9\,ms，良性写入后首次读取为1,188／1,095.1\,ms；两者版本来源也不同，不能单独归因于绑定开销。32个写连接下，16分片保留无触发器吞吐的96.6\%，单行为36.1\%。这些代价不构成联合安全性与吞吐曲线。}
