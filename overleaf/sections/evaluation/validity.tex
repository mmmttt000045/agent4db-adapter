\subsection{\bt{Validity under data changes}{数据变化下的有效性}}
\label{sec:validity}
\bi{\emph{Paired replay (Q1).} The earlier three-model scenario run (Section~\ref{sec:scenarios}) left each run with the library its agents learned: \RpLibs\ libraries of 3--8 promoted definitions, \RpDefs\ in all, from three LLMs. We replay every library under every method on the same data. Each method gets its own middleware instance, admitted with the library's definitions and learning evidence; each of the \RpChanges\ changes (the ten scenarios of Table~\ref{tab:scenarios} plus a backup-copy change, Section~\ref{sec:repair-eval}) is applied from the same initial snapshot; and the scored questions of every affected definition are answered with the canonical SQL of whatever revision the method serves. This isolates maintenance from learning and from agent behavior. A question is \emph{correct}, \emph{served wrong} (the served revision answers incorrectly), or \emph{unavailable}; unavailability is \emph{needed} if the unmaintained definition would also have answered incorrectly.}
{\emph{配对回放（Q1）。}此前一轮三模型场景实验（第~\ref{sec:scenarios} 节）在每次运行结束时留下该次运行学到的定义库：\RpLibs\ 个库，各含 3--8 个已晋升定义，共 \RpDefs\ 个，来自 3 个大模型。我们在同一份数据上用每种方法回放每个库。每种方法各有一个中间层实例，以该库的定义和学习证据准入；\RpChanges\ 种变化（表~\ref{tab:scenarios} 的 10 个场景，外加一个备份副本变化，见第~\ref{sec:repair-eval} 节）都从同一初始快照施加；每个受影响定义的计分题，用该方法当时提供的修订的规范 SQL 作答。这把维护与学习、与智能体行为隔离开。每道题的结果为\emph{答对}、\emph{提供但答错}（提供的修订答错）或\emph{不可用}；若未经维护的定义同样会答错，则称该不可用为\emph{必要的}。}

\bi{Table~\ref{tab:replay} shows the result. Every revalidation method serves wrong answers only under the unit change, which no condition models; under the modeled changes they serve none, whereas schema-only maintenance serves \RpSchemaWrongModeled\ wrong answers there and revoke-on-write without relearning answers nothing. Condition-level maintenance, definition-level revalidation, and the generic cache give the same outcome on every question (\RpSameCondDef\%), so differences between them in end-to-end accuracy come from what agents learned, not from maintenance. Unavailability that was not needed is conservative rather than wrong: it arises mostly for return rate under duplicate loads and late keys, where numerator and denominator inflate together and the ratio happens to stay right although the grain condition is broken.}
{表~\ref{tab:replay} 给出结果。各重验证方法只在任何条件都不刻画的单位变化下提供错误答案；在已建模的变化下一个也没有，而只看结构的维护在这些变化下提供了 \RpSchemaWrongModeled\ 个错误答案，不重新学习的写入即撤销则一题也答不了。条件级维护、定义级重验证与通用缓存在每道题上的结果都相同（\RpSameCondDef\%），因此它们在端到端正确率上的差别来自智能体学到的内容，而不是维护方式。不必要的不可用是保守而非错误：它主要出现在重复装载与迟到键下的退货率上，此时分子分母同步膨胀，比率碰巧仍然正确，但粒度条件确实被破坏了。}


\bi{\emph{Table tests.} The table-test baseline declares the tests a dbt project would: uniqueness of each fact table's business key and of each dimension key, and referential tests from the date and item keys, calibrated to pass on the initial snapshot. After every update it reruns them and quarantines every definition that reads a table with a failing test. It serves no wrong answer under the modeled changes either (\RpTableWrongModeled), because every modeled break also breaks a declared key, but it cannot repair: on the same questions it answers \RpTableCorrect\ against \RpCondCorrect\ for \system, and the \RpTableOnlyCond\ questions it loses are those \system\ repairs under the status history, versioned corrections, and dimension history, or keeps because the definition's own filter already satisfies the condition. Its unneeded unavailability (\RpTableUnneeded\ against \RpCondUnneeded) comes from acting on the table rather than on the definition.}
{\emph{表级测试。}表级测试基线声明的是 dbt 项目会声明的测试：各事实表业务键与各维表键的唯一性，以及日期键、商品键的引用测试，按初始快照校准。每次更新后它重跑全部测试，并隔离读到测试失败的表的所有定义。在已建模的变化下它同样不给出错误答案（\RpTableWrongModeled）——每种已建模的破坏都会破坏某个声明的键——但它不能修复：同一批题目上它答对 \RpTableCorrect\ 题，\system\ 答对 \RpCondCorrect\ 题，它少答对的 \RpTableOnlyCond\ 题，是 \system\ 在状态流水、版本化更正与维表拉链下修复的题目，或因定义自己的过滤已满足条件而保留的题目。它不必要的不可用（\RpTableUnneeded\ 题对 \RpCondUnneeded\ 题）来自按表而不是按定义行动。}

\bi{\emph{Real TPC-DS data.} The same replay runs on TPC-DS at scale factor 1 (2.9 million store-sales rows, all 24 tables as generated by dsdgen) with a library that was not written for this paper: the \TrDefs\ definitions that the derivation of Section~\ref{sec:alternatives} extracts from the 99 query templates and that the structured implementation can express (one aggregate over a sales or returns fact table with one date role, dimension joins from the fact table on primary keys, and single-table persistent filters); \TrSeeded\ pass admission and \TrSeedFailed\ have an empty learning period. The eleven changes are applied to the real tables through the same classes, copying complete rows and resolving dates through \code{date\_dim}; gold answers come from the definitions themselves with the distinguishing-column filters that the judged references of the synthetic fixture also carry. Over \TrCondN\ scored questions per method, the outcome classes match the synthetic fixture: \system\ serves no wrong answer under the modeled changes, repairs every affected definition under the status history, versioned corrections, and dimension history (\TrCondRepairedTasks\ questions answered through a repair), and withdraws definitions under duplicate loads, re-keyed dates, and the backup copy, where no unique filter restores the grain; schema-only maintenance serves \TrSchemaWrongModeled\ wrong answers, and table tests quarantine \TrTableNeeded\ needed and \TrTableUnneeded\ unneeded questions. The agent-side reference again refuses the repairs whose change touches the learning period, versioned corrections and dimension history: \TrPairEx\ of \TrPairN\ questions are answered against \TrPairJudge, and \TrPairExWrong\ become wrong. Maintenance time is dominated by the repair search under the duplicate load, which enumerates candidate filters over three million rows before withdrawing (\TrCondDuploadDB\ of \TrCondDB\,s).}
{\emph{真实 TPC-DS 数据。}同一回放也在规模因子 1 的 TPC-DS（290 万行门店销售，dsdgen 生成的全部 24 张表）上运行，定义库不是为本文编写的：第~\ref{sec:alternatives} 节的推导从 99 个查询模板中提取、且结构化实现能够表达的 \TrDefs\ 个定义（对一张销售或退货事实表的一个聚合，带一个日期角色、从事实表按维表主键发出的维表关联和单表持久过滤）；\TrSeeded\ 个通过准入，\TrSeedFailed\ 个学习期为空。11 种变化按相同的类别施加在真实表上，整行复制、日期经 \code{date\_dim} 换算；标准答案来自定义本身加上区分列过滤——与合成基准的判题参照所带的过滤相同。每种方法 \TrCondN\ 道计分题上的结果类别与合成数据一致：\system\ 在已建模的变化下不给出错误答案，在状态流水、版本化更正与维表拉链下修复了每一个受影响的定义（\TrCondRepairedTasks\ 道题经修复后作答），在重复装载、日期键改写与备份副本下撤下定义，因为没有唯一的过滤能恢复粒度；只看结构的维护给出 \TrSchemaWrongModeled\ 个错误答案，表级测试隔离了 \TrTableNeeded\ 道必要与 \TrTableUnneeded\ 道不必要的题。智能体侧参照同样拒绝变化触及学习期的修复（版本化更正与维表拉链）：\TrPairN\ 道配对题中答对 \TrPairEx\ 道（判题参照下 \TrPairJudge\ 道），变错的有 \TrPairExWrong\ 道。维护时间以重复装载下的修复搜索为主，它在 300 万行上逐个检查候选过滤后才撤下定义（\TrCondDuploadDB\ 秒，总计 \TrCondDB\ 秒）。}
